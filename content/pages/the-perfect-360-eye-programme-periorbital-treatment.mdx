<h2>What is the Perfect360™ Eye Programme?</h2>
<p>The Perfect360™ Eye Programme is a medical-grade, multi-modal treatment sequence. The delicate tissue surrounding the eyes ages uniquely because the periorbital zone comprises distinct anatomical layers, including skin, fat pads, muscle, and bone. Because these layers age at different rates, a single treatment rarely addresses all concerns. This programme combines advanced treatments such as:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Eye-Boost Hyaluronic Acid Filler:</strong> Our preferred Eye-Boost filler gently restores lost under-eye volume. It effectively softens tear trough hollows and reduces the shadowing that causes dark circles.</li>
<li><strong>Polynucleotides:</strong> Polynucleotides work at the cellular level to significantly improve periorbital skin quality. It stimulates deep tissue repair to restore hydration and elasticity to the delicate skin around the eyes.</li>
<li><strong>Skin Tightening:</strong> Energy-based skin tightening treatments safely tighten eyelid laxity without surgery. They are highly effective for managing crepey skin or providing a subtle tightening of lax eye bags.</li>
<li><strong>Muscle Relaxant Treatments:</strong> These targeted treatments provide an essential option for smoothing crow's feet by relaxing dynamic expression lines. As part of our comprehensive approach, they beautifully smooth the outer eye for a rested appearance.</li>
<li><strong>Bioregenerative injectables:</strong> Designed for the sensitive area around the eye, skin booster microinjections deliver intense hydration, amino acids, and vitamins. This powerful skin booster seamlessly smooths fine lines and supports lasting tissue rejuvenation.</li>
</ul>
<p>What sets this programme apart is the approach itself. Rather than offering a single treatment for a single concern, the Perfect360™ Eye Programme begins with a thorough assessment of every periorbital zone: under-eye, upper eyelid, brow, temple, and outer canthus.</p>

<h2>What Concerns Does The Perfect360™ Eye Programme Treat?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Tear trough hollowing:</strong> Sunken or shadowed under-eye areas caused by volume loss.</li>
<li><strong>Dark circles:</strong> Discolouration from thin skin, pigmentation, or visible vasculature.</li>
<li><strong>Crow's feet:</strong> Fine lines and creases at the outer corners of the eyes.</li>
<li><strong>Fine lines and crepey skin:</strong> Loss of elasticity and collagen around the eye area.</li>
<li><strong>Loss of brow support:</strong> Lowering of the brow contributes to a fatigued look.</li>
<li><strong>Puffiness:</strong> Fluid retention or fat pad displacement beneath the eyes.</li>
</ul>
