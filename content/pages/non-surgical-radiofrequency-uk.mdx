<h2>What is Radiofrequency Treatment?</h2>
<p>Radiofrequency (RF) treatment is a non-surgical procedure that uses controlled energy to gently heat the deeper layers of the skin. This heat stimulates collagen remodelling and may help improve skin firmness. It is sometimes used in the periocular area under medical supervision to manage mild skin laxity, hooding, or texture irregularity.</p>
<p>The treatment is not a substitute for surgical correction but may be considered for patients unsuitable or unwilling to undergo surgery.</p>
