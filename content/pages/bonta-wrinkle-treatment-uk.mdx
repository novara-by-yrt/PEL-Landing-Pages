<ul><li><b>Horizontal “Worry” lines (frontalis over-activity) –</b><span> lines that appear across the forehead and can sometimes remain visible.</span></li>
<li><b>“11’s” or Vertical “Frown” lines (corrugator over-activity) –</b><span> small lines that deepen over time between the brows from concentrating or frowning.</span></li>
<li><b>Laugh or Smile lines (orbicularis over-activity) –</b><span> crow’s feet that appear around the eyes when smiling or laughing</span></li>
<li><b>Jelly Rolls of lower eyelid (orbicularis hypertrophy)–</b><span> horizontal under-eye lines caused by blinking, squinting and unavoidable reflexes.</span></li>
<li><b>Bunny lines (procerus over-activity) –</b><span> tiny lines that appear when wrinkling the nose.</span></li>
<li><b>Bobbly / Golf-ball chin (mentalis over-activity) –</b><span> a dimpling effect on the chin due to excessive flexing.</span></li>
<li><b>Marionette Lines or labio-mental creases (DAO over-activity) –</b><span> lines on either side of the mouth that deepen over the years due to loss of facial volume.</span></li>
<li><b>Square jaw / Bruxism (masseter hypertrophy) –</b><span> masseter muscles that are over-pronounced in the jaw.</span></li>
<li><b>Turkey Neck (platysmal bands) –</b><span> when the thick muscle that runs from the ear to the collarbone pulls the lower face downwards over time, causing a sagging effect.</span></li>
<li><b>Brow ptosis or Asymmetric Brows –</b><span> when the brows start to drop or there is asymmetry.</span></li>
<li><b>Blepharospasm / Hemi-facial spasm –</b><span> muscular spasms around the eyes or involuntary contractions on one side of the face.</span></li>
</ul>
