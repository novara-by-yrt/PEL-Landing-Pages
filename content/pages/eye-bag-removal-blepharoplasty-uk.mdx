<h2>What is Eye Bag Removal Surgery (Lower Eyelid Blepharoplasty)?</h2>
<p>Lower-lid blepharoplasty is a surgical procedure performed to correct functional and structural issues in the lower eyelids, such as bulging fat, loose skin, or irritation from tissue prolapse. Fat pads are carefully repositioned or reduced, and only minimal skin refinement is performed where medically indicated to support lid function and ocular surface health.</p>
<p>This is a day-case procedure performed under general anaesthesia, tailored to each patient's anatomy and symptoms. While there are some excellent non-surgical options for mild cases, such as LASER and Tixel, Eye Bag surgery is the most effective way to treat moderate to severe eye bags.</p>

<h2>How Does Lower Lid Blepharoplasty Procedure Work?</h2>
<p>Eye bag removal surgery smooths the under-eye area by removing excess skin, bulging fat, and loose muscle from the lower eyelids. To do so, we make a tiny cut, which is either hidden in the natural lower lid crease or inside the lower lid, in the case of a &quot;Transconjunctival Blepharoplasty.&quot; The surgeon removes or repositions unwanted tissues before closing the incision with fine stitches. The skin can also be treated by laser resurfacing or a chemical peel to help tighten the skin of the lower lid.
