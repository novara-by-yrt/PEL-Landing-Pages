<h2>The Perfect360 Skin Package Includes</h2>

<h3>1. DNA Skin (a pin-prick test)</h3>
<p>DNA Skin is a genetic test that offers insight into key areas that influence skin ageing, offering personalised topical, nutraceutical, diet, and lifestyle recommendations for improved outcomes.</p>
<p>DNA Skin reports on your unique genetics - knowledge of your genes and genetic variations offers unparalleled insight into your biological systems, and how the gene variations that you carry could affect your skin and the way it ages.</p>
<h3>2. Skin Visia Analysis</h3>
<p>Visia accurately detects skin imperfections such as wrinkles, pores, hyperpigmentation, uneven texture, sun damage, bacteria, and oil plugs. The system also compares your skin to the skin of healthy people of similar age, gender, and ethnicity. This helps you understand the condition of your skin and aids you in making treatment choices.</p>
<h3>3. Wellness Consultation</h3>
<p>A remote 90-minute wellness consult with our Health &amp; Longevity coach Sandie, who will assist in personalising and optimising your lifestyle and diet to enhance your skin health.</p>
<h3>4. Doctor Consultation</h3>
<p>A remote or in-person consultation with one of the doctors in our team to discuss your unique genetic test results and offer advice and recommendations on oral supplements, a topical skincare regime, and personalised treatment plans for optimising and preventing or reversing the skin ageing changes that you are genetically coded for.</p>
<h3>5. Choice of Facial Treatment</h3>
<p>A choice of 3D MIRACL UltraClear laser facials (a course of 3 treatments, once a month) to improve the texture of skin - fine lines, wrinkles and hyperpigmentation - versus a course of Endermologie facial detoxifying lymphatic drainage facials (a course of 8 treatments, twice a month) to improve facial puffiness and restore a glow.</p>

<h2>A Lifetime of Optimal Skin Health Awaits You</h2>
<p>Your genes do not change, which means you will only need one genetic test. However, throughout your life, as your health and lifestyle factors impact your skin health journey, the Perfect Skin Studio and Dr Sabrina's team can continue to provide valuable insights and treatments to optimise your unique skin health.</p>
