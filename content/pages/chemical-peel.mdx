<h2>What is Chemical Peel?</h2>
<p>Chemical peel is applying a chemical solution to the skin, and it is used to eliminate the top layers of the skin. The skin that regenerates following this procedure tends to be smoother. Multiple sessions may be necessary for desired results with a light or medium peel. Chemical peels address wrinkles, bags, skin discolouration, and scars, often on the facial area. They may be performed alone or in conjunction with other treatments and can vary in depth from light to deep. While deeper chemical peels yield more significant outcomes, the recovery period is also longer.</p>

<h2>How Does Chemical Peel Work?</h2>
<p>A chemical peel is a procedure to resurface the skin. Depending on the concerns being addressed, a chemical peel can be light, medium, or deep:</p>
<p><b>Light chemical peel:</b> This removes the outer layer of skin (epidermis) and is used to address fine wrinkles, acne, uneven skin tone, &amp; dryness. It can be done every 2 to 5 weeks.</p>
<p><b>Medium chemical peel:</b> This removes skin cells from the epidermis and portions of the upper dermis. It is used to treat wrinkles, acne scars, &amp; uneven skin tone. It could take more than one procedure to get the desired result.</p>
<p><b>Deep chemical peel:</b> This removes skin cells even deeper. It's recommended for deeper wrinkles, scars, &amp; precancerous growths. The full effect is achieved without the need for repeat procedures.</p>
