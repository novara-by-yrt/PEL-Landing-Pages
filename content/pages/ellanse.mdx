<h2>How Does Biostimulation Filler Procedure Work?</h2>
<p><strong>Important Notes:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Results vary between individuals.</li>
<li>Any filler treatment carries potential risks, including swelling, tenderness, bruising, or delayed inflammatory reaction.</li>
<li>Patients are advised to undergo consultation and suitability assessment before treatment.</li>
</ul>
<img src="/uploads/2024/12/ellansefiller.jpg" alt="Biostimulation being injected during treatment" width="821" height="528" />
<p>Biostimulation contains smooth polycaprolactone (PCL) microspheres suspended in a carboxymethylcellulose (CMC) gel carrier. The gel provides immediate volumisation, while the microspheres act as a biostimulant, encouraging natural collagen synthesis over several months.</p>
<p>After applying a local anaesthetic, Dr Sabrina Shah-Desai or a qualified member of her team administers precise injections into the targeted areas to restore contour and balance.</p>
<p>This procedure is performed in a sterile medical setting and typically lasts about 30 minutes.</p>
<p>Patients are reviewed post-procedure to assess collagen response and ensure safe outcomes.</p>
