<p><span>There are three main types of brow lift surgeries:</span></p>
<p><span><b>Direct Brow Lift:</b> This is the minimally invasive type, requiring a tiny incision made around the brow's outer corner. It's suitable for patients with brow ptosis (drooping eyebrows) caused by facial nerve palsy or ageing.</span></p>
<p><span><b>Pretrichial Brow Lift:</b> This involves making incisions in front of the hairline, making it ideal for patients who comb their hair forward. This technique doesn't usually affect the hairline and can even lower it if needed.</span></p>
