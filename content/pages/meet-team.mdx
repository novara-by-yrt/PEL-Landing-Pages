<h2>Dr Michelle Emett</h2>
<p>A warm, caring doctor, Dr Michelle Emett has been practising medicine for 27 years and Aesthetic Medicine exclusively for 13 years.</p>
<p>After completing surgical primaries, she entered Accident and Emergency Medicine in South Africa and the UK. She spent six years as a Medical Advisor in Pharmaceuticals before finding her passion in Aesthetic Medicine. Like Dr Sabrina, Dr Emett also teaches aesthetic procedures at a senior national level and attends international congresses and trainings for updates. She has sat on many international panel discussions over the years.</p>
<p>Dr Michelle's professional relationship with Dr Sabrina spans many years, beginning when Dr Sabrina was invited to teach in South Africa as part of a Global Tour. Their continued collaboration and friendship are a testament to Dr Michelle's expertise and dedication to Aesthetic Medicine, providing reassurance to her potential patients.</p>
<p>Dr Michelle's passion for clinical facial anatomy is the cornerstone of her practice, underpinning her belief in the importance of sound scientific and aesthetic practice. Her dedication to precision aesthetics, which consistently delivers natural-looking results, instils confidence in her potential patients.</p>
<p>Cool, calm, and confident, Dr Michelle's expertise lies in Non-Surgical lifting and tightening procedures, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>Biostimulators: Ellanse, Sculptra</li>
<li>Biorejuvenation with Polynucleotides</li>
<li>Wrinkle-relaxing toxin injections</li>
<li>Full face dermal fillers</li>
<li>One-Stitch Mini Lift Silhouette Soft</li>
<li>Brow lifting facial threads</li>
<li>LASER (Vector Coring and Ablative with Ultraclear)</li>
<li>Profhilo &amp; Skinboosters</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Fun fact:</strong> Dr Michelle loves teaching, travel, and artistic anatomy.</p>

<h2>Dr Janine</h2>
<p>Dr Janine Rothburn is a highly skilled, advanced Aesthetic Doctor and Dental Surgeon (GDC number: 271191) who has been an aesthetic practitioner since 2018 and has been part of Dr Sabrina's team at Harley Street since 2021. She loves teaching and has been a mentor at Harley Academy since 2020.</p>
<p>Apart from her extensive knowledge of face and neck anatomy, Dr Sabrina has trained her in her specialist methods of facial assessment and aesthetic injectable techniques.</p>
<p>In addition, she has been trained in facial ultrasonography to offer vascular mapping and targeted dissolution of facial fillers.</p>
<p>Dr Janine offers a holistic approach to her patients. She combines her expertise in the assessment of the perioral area with an artistic approach during the treatment. This allows her to create natural and subtle results, instilling confidence in her patients and ensuring their satisfaction.</p>
<p>Dr Janine is committed to providing our patients with the best level and quality of service. She follows a professional and pleasant approach to make sure that everyone has a safe and comfortable experience.</p>
<p>Aligned with Dr Sabrina's philosophy, Dr Janine believes in the power of customised treatments. This approach ensures that each patient's unique aesthetic needs are understood and addressed, leaving them feeling naturally rejuvenated and refreshed.</p>
<p><strong>Treatments Offered:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Wrinkle Relaxing treatments with neurotoxin</li>
<li>Dermal fillers for facial contouring and sculpting (Jawline, Chin, Lip, Cheek &amp; Temple Fillers)</li>
<li>Ellanse for biostimulation</li>
<li>Profhilo &amp; Skin boosters for the face, neck, décolletage, and hands</li>
<li>Polynucleotides for eye and face skin regeneration and rejuvenation</li>
<li>Sofwave Ultrasound for brow lifts, face, and neck skin tightening</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> Dr Janine loves to travel.</p>

<h2>Dr Rosa Santa Cruz</h2>
<p>Dr Rosa Santa Cruz is a longstanding Harley Street based Consultant Surgeon with an entry on the Specialist Register for General Surgery (GMC number: 7441197), who mainly specialises in aesthetic and regenerative medicine.</p>
<p>Dr Santa Cruz's MD thesis was awarded for original research in computerised morphometry of breast cancer, and she has published papers regarding thyroid conditions, pulmonary fibrosis and predictions of post-operative outcomes. Her background also includes a PhD in Natural Sciences, a doctorate in Forensic Sciences with a thesis in rare genetic conditions, and working abroad (USA, Italy and Spain).</p>
<p>She has worked at Chelsea and Westminster Hospital, and at Watford General Hospital, in London.</p>
<p>Like Dr Sabrina, she shares the same passion and dedication for innovation and bespoke medical therapies and treatments designed to deliver the safest, least painful and most effective outcomes to their patients, always with the minimal possible downtime.</p>
<p>Regenerative Medicine is Dr Rosa's main professional interest and focus: PRP, i-PRF, Microvesicles, Exosomes, Stem Cells and derivatives, and Polynucleotides.</p>
<p><strong>Treatments offered:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Tailored medical therapies for skin conditions including rejuvenation, hyperpigmentation, acne, melasma, scars, and skin tightening</li>
<li>Biostimulation with PRP (Platelet Rich Plasma), Exosomes, i-PRF</li>
<li>PRP / Exosomes for hair restoration</li>
<li>Customised combined treatments for skin rejuvenation</li>
<li>Tear trough correction / eye area rejuvenation with i-PRF / Polynucleotides</li>
<li>Skin boosters</li>
<li>Profhilo Hyaluronic acid injections</li>
<li>Mesotherapy</li>
<li>DNA skin testing</li>
<li>Skincare consultations</li>
