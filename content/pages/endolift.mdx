<h2>How Does the Endolift® Procedure Work?</h2>
<p>Less downtime: compared to surgical alternatives. Long-lasting results: for up to 2 years. Unrivalled expertise: performed by a leading oculoplastic surgeon.</p>
<img src="/uploads/2024/12/endolift-1.jpg" alt="Endolift laser fibre being inserted during treatment" width="960" height="540" />
<p>Endolift® therapy uses a 1470nm laser and operates using single-use micro-optical fibres, which are as thin as hair and easily inserted under the skin into the superficial hypodermis. The laser energy targets water and fat explicitly, effectively liquefying the fat while stimulating collagen production. This leads to immediate skin retraction and reduces skin laxity. Usually, only one treatment is recommended, which takes 1 to 2 hours and is performed under local anaesthesia. The full results of Endolift® take 3 to 6 months to become fully evident, although this timeline can vary by patient, with some individuals seeing visible results sooner. It is undertaken after clinical assessment to confirm suitable lid support and to exclude conditions such as festoons or fluid oedema. After the Endolift® treatment, you will receive aftercare instructions, and an appointment will be scheduled for your initial review between 4 to 6 weeks. We suggest that you arrange for someone to pick you up from the clinic.</p>
