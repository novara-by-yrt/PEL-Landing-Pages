<h2>How Does Double Eyelid Blepharoplasty Procedure Work?</h2>
<p>No scar: the cut is hidden in the natural eyelid crease, or not required if using a running stitch. Long-lasting results: from 2 to 10 years. Unrivalled expertise: performed by a leading oculoplastic surgeon.</p>
<img src="/uploads/2024/07/woman-getting-face-marked.png" alt="Patient being marked up before double eyelid surgery" width="822" height="529" />
<p>Double Eyelid Surgery can create a &quot;wide awake&quot; appearance by forming a natural crease in the upper lids and removing excess skin, muscle, and fat. Double Eyelid Surgery, or an Asian Blepharoplasty, can be performed in two ways.</p>
<p><b>Incision/Open Technique:</b> This uses a cut, which enables the scar to be hidden in the crease and requires approximately 2 weeks of downtime, with the scar being fully settled by 3-6 months. The procedure typically lasts 5-15 years.</p>
<p><b>Non-Incision/Suture Technique:</b> This uses a running stitch to create the crease and requires no incisions. Therefore, it does not leave a scar. The recovery is faster, approximately 1-2 weeks. However, the procedure typically lasts only 2-5 years.</p>
<p>Double Eyelid Surgery is performed under local anaesthesia or twilight anaesthesia as a day case, so you can go home on the same day.</p>
