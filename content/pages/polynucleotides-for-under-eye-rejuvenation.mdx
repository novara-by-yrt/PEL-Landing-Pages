<h2>What Are Polynucleotides?</h2>
<p>Polynucleotides are fragments of Fish DNA in a saline injectable gel, that aims to help repair tissue by stimulating fibroblasts, encouraging cell turnover, improving elasticity, boosting collagen production &amp; hydration of the skin. For those who are susceptible to swelling post conventional dermal fillers or are scared of complications associated with dermal fillers and have very thin under eye skin are ideal candidates. Polynucleotides enhance the skin's quality by encouraging the body's natural regenerative processes.</p>
<p>When Polynucleotide's are injected into the skin, they stimulate the production of collagen and elastin, which help to improve the texture and elasticity of the skin. Polynucleotides also enhance the skin's natural ability to retain moisture, resulting in a more hydrated and youthful appearance.</p>
