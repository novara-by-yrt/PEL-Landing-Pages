<h2>What is UltraClear Laser (CoolBleph)?</h2>
<p>UltraClear is the first cold ablative fibre-optic Erbium laser approved by the FDA for all skin types. CoolBleph with UltraClear targets a wide range of skin concerns, with a strong focus on non-surgical eyelid rejuvenation, in a single session. It helps to resurface, refresh, and even out your skin tone safely and precisely.</p>
<p>This advanced UltraClear CoolBleph treatment helps remove damaged tissue, even out pigmentation, and stimulate collagen. The result is firmer, smoother skin with a refreshed eye area and minimal downtime.</p>

<h2>How UltraClear Laser (CoolBleph) Works</h2>
<p>UltraClear laser resurfacing treatment helps remove damaged tissue and stimulate collagen. The 2910nm wavelength technology makes it appropriate for all skin types because it enables precise control over the targeted tissue. This helps prevent heat buildup with a fast, thin LASER light beam. This technology offers multiple benefits over traditional LASER treatments by reducing pain, discomfort, downtime, and the risk of serious skin injury. This helps the skin to renew itself and build new skin cells. It also stimulates the production of collagen and elastin, helping firm your eyelids and face. This makes the skin smoother, healthier, and younger.</p>
