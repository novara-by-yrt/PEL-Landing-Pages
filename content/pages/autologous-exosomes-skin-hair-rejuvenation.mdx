<h2>What are Autologous Exosomes?</h2>
<span>Autologous exosomes are exosomes derived from an individual's own body, making them "self-derived" or "self-sourced." These exosomes are obtained from the person's tissues/cells and then used for various therapeutic or regenerative purposes.
