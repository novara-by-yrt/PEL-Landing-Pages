    <div class="section-header center">
      <span class="eyebrow accent">The Procedure</span>
      <h2>What is Upper Eyelid Lift Surgery?</h2>
      <p class="section-sub">
        Upper Lid Blepharoplasty, also referred to as &ldquo;Eyelid Lift Surgery,&rdquo; is an eyelid surgical procedure that particularly addresses the upper eyelids. An upper eyelid blepharoplasty focuses on the area between the eyebrow and the lash line and targets problems like sagging or redundant skin, bulging fat, and drooping muscles, which create a hooded appearance and obstruct peripheral vision. Eyelid lift surgery involves carefully making incisions inside the eyelid&rsquo;s natural folds, performed under local anaesthesia or twilight anaesthesia.
      </p>
    </div>

  <div class="container split-layout reverse">
    <div class="split-content">
      <span class="eyebrow accent">The Surgical Approach</span>
      <h2>How Does Eyelid Lift Surgery Procedure Work?</h2>
      <p>
        After careful assessment, the surgeon marks the natural eyelid crease and removes a tailored amount of redundant skin and, if indicated, a fine strip of muscle or fat. Every effort is made to preserve tissue that supports normal closure and natural contour.
      </p>
      <p>
        The incision is closed with delicate sutures and covered with light dressings. Patients use lubricating drops and cold compresses in the first few days to reduce swelling and dryness. Most return to desk-based activity within several days as advised.
      </p>
      <p>
        The surgery is performed by an oculoplastic surgeon, Dr Shah-Desai, who uses a signature "Zip Stitch" technique to minimise scarring.
      </p>

<section class="faq">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-header">
      <span class="eyebrow accent">FAQs</span>
      <h2>Patient Questions About Upper-Lid Blepharoplasty</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="faq-list">
      <details class="faq-item" open>
        <summary>What is the recovery time following Eyelid Lift Surgery?</summary>
        <p>Initial recovery generally takes about one week. Stitches are removed after 7-10 days, and most patients resume desk-based activity soon after. Bruising and mild swelling may persist for two to three weeks.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Will Eyelid Lift Surgery feminise male eyelids?</summary>
        <p>No. The aim is to restore comfortable lid function and remove redundant tissue while preserving each patient's natural eyelid shape and brow position. In men, care is taken to maintain their typical T-shaped brow and avoid an overly lifted or feminised appearance.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>What are the common risks of Eyelid Lift Surgery?</summary>
        <p>Bruising, swelling, transient dryness, or irritation are common and usually settle within a few weeks. Other risks include temporary blurred vision, mild asymmetry, or visible scarring (rare and typically minimal). All risks and aftercare are discussed during consultation.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Will my surgical scars be visible afterwards?</summary>
        <p>Incisions are hidden within the eyelid's natural crease and usually fade over several months. Visible scarring is uncommon.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Can I start reading right after surgery?</summary>
        <p>Vision may be slightly blurred for a few days due to swelling or the use of eye lubricants. Reading and other visual tasks can be resumed as comfort allows.</p>
      </details>
    </div>
  </div>
