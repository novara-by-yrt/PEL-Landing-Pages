Dubbed "not just a facial" it is an hour long deep cleansing and detoxifying experience. The HydraFacial utilises 6 sequential steps starting with lymphatic drainage, microdermabrasion, peels, extraction, infusion, hydration and light emitting diode collagen stimulating treatments, to cleanse, exfoliate, detoxify, extract, hydrate and protect the skin. It is not painful or irritating, making it safe even for sensitive skin and an effective treatment for all skin types of any age.</p>
<p>Skin is left looking and feeling fresher with an overall improvement in skin appearance, texture and hydration. Perfect for glowing healthy skin, these treatments are popular with brides & grooms, celebrities, dermatologists and teenagers.</p>

You then receive the same refreshing serum used in your treatment as a take-home product.</p>
