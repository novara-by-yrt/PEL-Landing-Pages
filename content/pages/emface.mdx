<h2>How Does EMFACE Procedure Work?</h2>
<p>Instant result: improvement can be seen immediately and final results take 3 months. No downtime: patients can return to work right away. Unrivalled expertise: performed under the care of a leading oculoplastic surgeon and her trained team.</p>
<img src="/uploads/2025/03/emface-4.png" alt="EMFACE applicators in use during treatment" width="960" height="640" />
<p>An EMFACE procedure works by combining two advanced technologies:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>HIFES (High-Intensity Facial Electromagnetic Stimulation):</strong> Tones and lifts the facial muscles, similar to how body sculpting treatments strengthen abs or glutes.</li>
<li><strong>Radiofrequency (RF) Energy:</strong> Heats the skin's deeper layers, triggering collagen and elastin production to smooth wrinkles and firm sagging skin.</li>
</ul>
<p>During treatment, small applicators are placed either on the forehead and around the eyes, or eyes and double chin area, or midface and double chin, we can combine two areas at a time simultaneously (except eyes and midface which we can do sequentially). You'll feel a mild warmth and gentle muscle contractions, similar to a facial massage. The procedure takes just 20 minutes, and there's zero downtime—you can return to work or social activities right away.</p>
