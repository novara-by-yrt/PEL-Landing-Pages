<h2>What is the Perfect360™ Skin Programme with Injectable Skin Boosters?</h2>
<p>The Perfect360™ Skin Programme is a medical-grade, multi-layered treatment approach using four advanced injectable skin boosters: Sunekos®, Redensity 1, Profhilo®, and Polynucleotides.</p>
<p>As the skin ages, or following lifestyle changes such as weight loss or stress, multiple biological processes slow down. Collagen reduces, elastin weakens, hydration drops, and the skin's ability to repair itself diminishes. Treating just one of these rarely creates meaningful or lasting improvement. This programme combines regenerative treatments to address the full picture:</p>

<h3>Sunekos® Injection (Collagen &amp; Elastin Regeneration)</h3>
<p>Sunekos® is a skin-regeneration injection that combines hyaluronic acid with a patented amino acid formula to stimulate collagen and elastin production. It improves skin quality at a structural level rather than adding volume, making it one of the few injectables safe and effective for delicate areas such as the lower eyelid.</p>
<p>Sunekos 200 is used for early signs of ageing and dehydration. Sunekos 1200 supports deeper lines and more advanced loss of elasticity.</p>

<h3>Redensity 1 (Dermal Environment Optimisation)</h3>
<p>Redensity 1 by Teoxane combines hyaluronic acid with amino acids and antioxidants to improve dermal hydration and support cellular function. It is the foundation of the babyGLOW by Teoxane skin programme and works as an ideal baseline or maintenance treatment for dull, dehydrated, or stressed skin.</p>

<h3>Profhilo® (Bio-Remodelling for Laxity)</h3>
<p>Profhilo® delivers a high concentration of hyaluronic acid to bio-remodel the skin, improving firmness and elasticity across larger surface areas. It is best suited to treating skin laxity and loss of firmness, not fine lines or thin skin, and not precision areas such as the lower eyelid.</p>

<h3>Polynucleotides (Cellular Repair &amp; Anti-Inflammatory Regeneration)</h3>
<p>Polynucleotides use DNA fragments to activate fibroblasts, support tissue repair, and reduce inflammation and oxidative stress. They are particularly effective in complex periorbital cases, including patients with prior filler complications or chronically compromised skin.</p>
<p>What makes this programme different is the approach itself. Instead of treating a single concern, we assess the skin as a whole, identify the dominant ageing pathway, and deliver treatment in stages, allowing the skin to adapt naturally.</p>

<h2>What Concerns Does the Perfect360™ Injectable Skin Booster Programme Treat?</h2>
<p>This programme is designed to improve overall skin quality and address multiple concerns at once.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fine lines and early wrinkles.</strong> As collagen reduces, fine lines begin to appear. Sunekos® treatment helps rebuild these structural fibres, softening lines and improving skin smoothness over time.</li>
<li><strong>Skin laxity and reduced firmness.</strong> Loss of elasticity can make the skin appear looser or less supported. Profhilo®, when combined with Sunekos® or Polynucleotides where appropriate, gradually restores strength and resilience by stimulating collagen and elastin.</li>
<li><strong>Dehydration and dullness.</strong> Skin that lacks hydration often appears tired and uneven. Redensity 1 and the amino acid component of Sunekos® improve deep hydration, enhancing radiance and texture.</li>
<li><strong>Thin or fragile skin.</strong> In areas where the skin has become delicate, particularly around the eyes, Sunekos® injections improve thickness and quality, making the skin more durable and resilient over time.</li>
<li><strong>Inflammation and compromised skin.</strong> For patients with reactive, post-procedure, or chronically stressed skin, Polynucleotides provide targeted anti-inflammatory and regenerative support before or alongside other treatments.</li>
<li><strong>Early ageing or preventative concerns.</strong> This treatment is also suitable for patients looking to maintain skin quality and prevent early signs of ageing from becoming more noticeable.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Sunekos vs Profhilo: Understanding the Difference</h2>
<p>Patients often compare Sunekos® vs Profhilo®, as both are injectable skin boosters used to improve overall skin quality.</p>
<p>Profhilo® focuses on delivering a high concentration of hyaluronic acid to improve hydration and spread through the skin. It is effective for overall laxity and firmness, but does not directly target elastin production and is not suited to precision areas.</p>
<p>Sunekos®, as an amino acid skin booster, combines hyaluronic acid with a patented amino acid formulation to actively regenerate both collagen and elastin. This makes it particularly effective for improving skin structure and elasticity, as well as delicate areas such as the under-eye area, where precision is essential.</p>
<p>In many cases, the most effective approach is not a choice between the two, but a structured combination of both alongside Redensity 1 and Polynucleotides tailored to your specific skin condition and goals. Choosing the right treatment depends on your skin's dominant concerns, which is why a personalised consultation is essential.</p>
