<h2>What is JULÄINE™ Treatment?</h2>
<p>JULÄINE™ is a state-of-the-art collagen biostimulator that works through injections and is meant to improve skin quality by natural means. This is achieved by stimulating your body's own collagen production. Unlike the usual dermal fillers that create instant volume, JULÄINE™ gradually advances skin renewal, resulting in more natural, fresher-looking skin over time.</p>
<p>The procedure uses Poly-L-Lactic Acid (PLLA) as the main ingredient, a biocompatible, biodegradable material that has been used safely in medical applications for many years. When injected directly into specific areas of the skin, JULÄINE™ stimulates fibroblast activity, leading to the formation of new collagen and improving skin structure.</p>
<p>JULÄINE™ is well-suited for achieving skin with greater firmness, elasticity, and smoothness, without altering facial features or creating an overfilled look. The differences manifest gradually, so the skin appears healthier, smoother, and more radiant in a subtle yet balanced way.</p>
