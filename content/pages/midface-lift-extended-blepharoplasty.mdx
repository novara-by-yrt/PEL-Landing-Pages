<h2>What is an Extended Blepharoplasty and Mid Facelift?</h2>
<p>A lower lid blepharoplasty that is combined with a lateral SOOF mid facelift is a minimally invasive, surgical treatment. Patients with severe, ongoing malar mounds and festoons that are recurrent or chronic usually choose this procedure. This procedure has the benefit of simultaneously treating malar bags and eye bags.</p>
<p>The goal of a midface lift treatment is to improve the alignment between the eyelid and cheek while also realigning the prominent appearance and position of the cheekbones. In order to give the patient more fullness around the eyes and cheeks and to help them look younger, more rested, and more refreshed, the midface lift can help raise the cheek fat pad.</p>
