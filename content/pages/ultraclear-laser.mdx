<h2>What is Ultraclear Laser?</h2>
<p>Ultraclear is the world's first cold ablative fibre-optic Erbium laser that has been FDA approved for treatment in all skin types. It restores and rejuvenates skin health by resurfacing skin and removing uneven pigmentation, whilst stimulating collagen and elastin regeneration to smooth out fine lines and wrinkles and improve skin texture.</p>

<h2>Treatment</h2>
<p>It is suitable for all skin types, as the 2910nm wavelength technology allows a very fast and thin beam of laser light to finely control the amount of tissue targeted, preventing heat accumulation. This is the advantage over traditional laser treatments as it minimises pain, discomfort, downtime and recovery time and risk of serious injury to the skin.</p>
