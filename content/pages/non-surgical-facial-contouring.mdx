<h2>What is Non Surgical Facial Contouring?</h2>
<p>Face contouring is now a popular way to achieve a "sculpted" look with a defined jawline and defined cheekbones. Suitable for all ages, this non-invasive treatment is painless.</p>
<p>Non-surgical facial contouring with dermal fillers is a great way to achieve a more youthful facial profile and appearance without the cost and downtime of surgery. Precisely placed hyaluronic acid filler can support sagging brows, mask heavy jowls, plump nasolabial folds, and more. These fillers also shape your face as well as stimulate collagen production and hydrate your skin, so you end up with improved quality skin and an impeccable glow.</p>

<h2>Non Surgical Treatments for Facial Contouring</h2>
<h3>Dermal Fillers</h3>
<p>Dermal fillers add volume and shape to areas like cheeks, jawline, or lips by injecting hyaluronic acid or similar substances under the skin.</p>

<h3>Non-Surgical Facelift</h3>
<p>This treatment uses a combination of fillers, or ultrasound-based therapies, to lift and tighten sagging skin without surgery. In a non-surgical facelift, Dr Shah-Desai and her team strategically place filler at anatomical points to gently and naturally support the face by addressing localised volume loss.</p>
