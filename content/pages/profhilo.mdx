<h1>Ageing face, neck, hands, decolletage and Profhilo treatments– by Leading Oculoplastic Surgeon, Dr Sabrina Shah-Desai, in London</h1>
<span>Profhilo is a revolutionary injectable treatment which uses a high concentration of hyaluronic acid to boost and hydrate the skin, while also remodelling sagging or ageing tissues. Since launching in 2015, it has won numerous awards for its innovativeness, as well as its natural-looking results. Profhilo is suitable for both men and women of all ages and skin types, while the most common areas for treatment include the face, neck, decolletage, back of hands, arms, knees and abdomen.</span>Dr Sabrina/ her team starts by cleaning the skin and mapping out the targeted injection sites ahead of treatment (for optimal results, the face requires five points on each side). Topical anaesthetic is not required for treatment – however, if you are concerned about discomfort, this can easily be applied. The hyaluronic acid-based gel is then injected into the dermis, using a very fine needle, where it not only restores hydration but it also stimulates the fibroblasts to produce fresh collagen and elastin – for renewed firmness. Immediately after, you will notice small “bee sting” welts, which will disappear in less than 24 hours. Two treatments, four weeks apart, are required – resulting in dewy, luminous, healthy-looking skin. The whole procedure takes around 20 minutes.
