      - "Upper lid Blepharoplasty, also referred to as \"Eyelid Lift Surgery,\" is an eyelid surgical procedure that particularly addresses the upper eyelids. An upper eyelid blepharoplasty focuses on the area between the eyebrow and the lash line and targets problems like sagging or redundant skin, bulging fat, and drooping muscles, which create a hooded appearance and obstruct peripheral vision. Eyelid lift surgery involves carefully making incisions inside the eyelid's natural folds, performed under local anaesthesia or twilight anaesthesia."
      - "Surgery to improve the appearance of the eyelids is called blepharoplasty. The goal of blepharoplasty is to give the eyes a more youthful look by removing excess skin, bulging fat and lax muscle from the upper lids. If the sagging skin obstructs peripheral vision, blepharoplasty can eliminate the obstruction and expand the visual field. Excess skin and fat are removed from the upper lid through a cut hidden in the natural eyelid crease. If the upper lid is droopy, the muscle that raises the lid can be tightened. The cut is then closed with fine stitches."

<h2>How Does Eyelid Lift Surgery Procedure Work?</h2>
<p>Invisible scar: the cut is hidden in the natural eyelid crease. Long-lasting results: up to 10 years. Unmatched expertise: performed by a highly skilled oculoplastic surgeon.</p>
<img src="/uploads/2025/12/Image-01.jpg" alt="Dr Sabrina Shah-Desai performing upper eyelid blepharoplasty" width="720" height="522" />
<p>After careful assessment, the surgeon marks the natural eyelid crease and removes a tailored amount of redundant skin and, if indicated, a fine strip of muscle or fat. Every effort is made to preserve tissue that supports normal closure and natural contour.</p>
<p>The incision is closed with delicate sutures and covered with light dressings. Patients use lubricating drops and cold compresses in the first few days to reduce swelling and dryness. Most return to desk-based activity within several days as advised.</p>
<p>The surgery is performed by an oculoplastic surgeon, Dr Shah-Desai, who uses a signature "Zip Stitch" technique to minimise scarring.</p>
