<h2>What are Polynucleotides?</h2>
<p>Polynucleotides are fragments of Fish DNA in a saline injectable gel that aim to help repair tissue by stimulating fibroblasts, encouraging cell turnover, improving elasticity, and boosting collagen production and hydration of the skin. Those who are susceptible to swelling after conventional dermal fillers or are concerned about complications associated with dermal fillers, and have very thin under-eye skin are ideal candidates. Polynucleotides enhance the skin's quality by encouraging the body's natural regenerative processes.</p>

<h2>How Does the Polynucleotide Procedure Work?</h2>
<p><b>1. Preparation:</b> Topical numbing cream is applied for 15 minutes.</p>
<p><b>2. Injection:</b> Using a fine needle or cannula, Polynucleotides are carefully administered into the under-eye tissue.</p>
<p><b>3. Mechanism:</b> Polynucleotides signal cells to stimulate collagen, hyaluronic acid, and tissue regeneration, supporting gradual improvement in skin quality.</p>
