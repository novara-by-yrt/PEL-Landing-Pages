<h2>What is Microneedling Treatment?</h2>
<p>Microneedling, also known as collagen induction therapy, is a minimally invasive procedure designed to support dermal health and improve skin structure.</p>
<p>It can be used to address mild skin laxity, atrophic textural change, and uneven tone in the periocular area. The treatment works by creating precise micro-channels in the superficial dermis, triggering collagen and elastin formation to support skin integrity and smoothness.</p>
<p>By adjusting the depth and density of treatment, clinicians can safely treat delicate under-eye skin while maintaining the protective barrier function.</p>
