<ul><li>Horizontal “Worry” lines (frontalis over-activity) – lines that appear across the forehead and can sometimes remain visible.</li>
 	<li>“11’s” or Vertical “Frown” lines (corrugator over-activity) – small lines that deepen overtime between the brows from concentrating or frowning.</li>
 	<li>Laugh or Smile lines (orbicularis over-activity) – crow’s feet that appear around the eyes when smiling or laughing</li>
 	<li>Jelly Rolls of lower eyelid (orbicularis hypertrophy)– horizontal under-eye lines caused by blinking, squinting and unavoidable reflexes.</li>
 	<li>Bunny lines (procerus over-activity) – tiny lines that appear when wrinkling the nose.</li>
 	<li>Bobbly / Golf-ball chin (mentalis over-activity) – a dimpling effect on the chin due to excessive flexing.</li>
 	<li>Marionette Lines or labio-mental creases (DAO over-activity) – lines on either side of the mouth that deepen over the years due to loss of facial volume.</li>
 	<li>Square jaw / Bruxism (masseter hypertrophy) – masseter muscles that are over-pronounced in the jaw.</li>
 	<li>Turkey Neck (platysmal bands) – when the thick muscle that runs from the ear to the collarbone pulls the lower face downwards over time, causing a sagging effect.</li>
 	<li>Brow ptosis or Asymmetric Brows – when the brows start to drop or there is asymmetry.</li>
 	<li>Blepharospasm / Hemi-facial spasm – muscular spasms around the eyes or involuntary contractions on one side of the face.</li>
</ul>

As with any drug, there are some possible side effects. However, the product only stays in the body for a maximum of 6 months, so any side effects associated with the injection will diminish after that time. Possible side effects can include:
<ul><li><strong>Bruising and or erythema:</strong> redness of the skin at the site of injection.</li>
 	<li><strong>Feeling unwell:</strong> headache, respiratory infections, nausea and flu-like symptoms.</li>
 	<li><strong>Temporary Ptosis or droopy eyelid:</strong> this typically reverses in 4-6 weeks as the toxin begins to wear off. It can be managed with Apraclonidine and Phenylephrine drops three times a day for a few weeks.</li>
 	<li><strong>The “Spock look”:</strong> lateral brow peaking can be addressed with a small amount of further treatment at the apex of the peak.</li>
 	<li><strong>Droopy, flat brow or a lower resting eyebrow position:</strong> this can occur when the forehead is treated to eliminate horizontal forehead lines. The risk increases with age and can be minimized by microdroplets.</li>
 	<li><strong>Temporary lower facial paralysis:</strong> this is an effect of the chemical neuromodulator diffusing further than the treatment dose and can be minimized by microdroplet.</li>
</ul>
