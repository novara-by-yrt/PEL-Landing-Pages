<h2>What is Eyelid Lump & Bump Removal?</h2>
<p>Eyelid lumps &amp; bumps can be unsightly and uncomfortable, affecting both the appearance and health of your eyes. There are various types of eyelid lump and bump removal surgeries, such as Papillomas, xanthelasma removal, chalazion (stye) removal and cyst removal. Eyelid bumps like styes or chalazion occur when the eyelid gland that produces oil to lubricate the edges of the eyes becomes clogged. Our Eyelid Lump &amp; Bump Removal treatment is designed to safely and effectively remove these unwanted growths, restoring the natural look and feel of your eyelids.</p>

<h2>How Does Eyelid Bump & Lump Treatment Work?</h2>
<p>First, you can try treating your eyelid bump at home if it is a stye or chalazion. Use a warm compress and gently massage the area to help clear the oily blockage. You can also use diluted baby shampoo to clean the surface. Usually, these bumps will go away by themselves in 2 weeks. If home treatments don't work, we will examine your eyelids to determine the nature of your eyelid lump or bump. If the bump is quite big, we might need to drain it. We will numb the area with a local anaesthetic before making a small cut inside the eyelid (so no visible scar). The procedure is quick and often completed within 10 minutes. Post-procedure care is straightforward, with most patients resuming normal activities within a few days.</p>
