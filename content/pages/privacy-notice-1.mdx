<h1>Privacy Notice</h1>

<h3>1. Who We Are</h3>
<p><strong>Perfect Eyes Ltd</strong> is the Data Controller for the purposes of UK data protection legislation.<br />
We are registered with the <strong>Information Commissioner’s Office</strong> (ICO) under registration number: <strong>ZA280470</strong>.</p>
<h3>2. What Personal Data We Collect</h3>
<p class="bottom-space-para">Identity and Contact Information:</p>
<ul><li>Name</li>
<li>Date of birth</li>
<li>Address</li>
<li>Email address</li>
<li>Telephone number</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">Clinical Information (Special Category Data):</p>
<ul><li>Medical history</li>
<li>Examination findings</li>
<li>Treatment records</li>
<li>Prescriptions</li>
<li>Clinical photographs</li>
<li>Correspondence with other healthcare professionals</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">Financial Information:</p>
<ul><li>Billing information</li>
<li>Payment details (processed via secure third-party providers)</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">Marketing Preferences:</p>
<ul><li>Communication preferences</li>
<li>Newsletter subscriptions</li>
</ul>
<h3>3. Special Category Data</h3>
<p>As a specialist medical practice, we process special category data (including health information) in order to provide safe and appropriate medical care.</p>
<h3>4. Our Lawful Basis for Processing</h3>
<p class="bottom-space-para">For Clinical Care:</p>
<ul><li>Article 6(1)(e) - Public task (healthcare provision)</li>
<li>Article 9(2)(h) - Provision of health or social care</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">For Contractual Services:</p>
<ul><li>Article 6(1)(b) - Performance of a contract</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">For Legal and Regulatory Compliance:</p>
<ul><li>Article 6(1)(c) - Legal obligation</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">For Marketing Communications:</p>
<ul><li>Article 6(1)(a) - Consent (which you may withdraw at any time)</li>
</ul>
<h3>5. How We Use Your Information</h3>
<p class="bottom-space-para">We use your information to:</p>
<ul><li>Provide medical consultations and treatment</li>
<li>Maintain accurate clinical records</li>
<li>Communicate with other healthcare professionals (where clinically appropriate)</li>
<li>Arrange appointments and follow-up care</li>
<li>Process payments</li>
<li>Comply with legal and regulatory obligations</li>
</ul>
<h3>6. Clinical Photography</h3>
<p>Clinical photographs form part of your medical record and are retained in accordance with the same retention schedule as other clinical records.<br />
Separate explicit consent is obtained before any identifiable images are used for teaching, publication, or marketing purposes.</p>
<h3>7. Data Sharing</h3>
<p class="bottom-space-para">We may share relevant information with:</p>
<ul><li>Your GP or other healthcare professionals (where clinically appropriate)</li>
<li>Laboratories or diagnostic providers</li>
<li>Professional advisers (e.g., insurers, legal advisers)</li>
<li>Regulatory authorities where required</li>
</ul>
<p>We use carefully selected third-party data processors including practice management software providers, secure cloud storage providers, payment processing providers, and IT support providers. All processors are contractually bound to maintain confidentiality and data security.</p>
<h3>8. International Transfers</h3>
<p>Some third-party service providers may process data outside the UK or EEA.<br />
Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including UK adequacy regulations, International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs), or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).</p>
<h3>9. How Long We Keep Your Personal Data</h3>
<p class="bottom-space-para">We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for clinical, legal, regulatory, accounting, and insurance purposes.</p>
<p class="bottom-space-para">Clinical Records:</p>
<ul><li>Adult patient records are retained for a minimum of 8 years from the date of last treatment.</li>
<li>Records may be retained for longer where there is an ongoing complaint, claim, regulatory requirement, or clinical justification.</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">Financial Records:</p>
<ul><li>Retained for at least 6 years in accordance with HMRC requirements.</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">Marketing Data:</p>
<ul><li>Retained until you withdraw consent or request removal.</li>
</ul>
<p class="bottom-space-para">CCTV (if applicable):</p>
<ul><li>Typically retained for 30 days unless required for investigation purposes.</li>
</ul>
<p>We maintain a documented Data Retention and Disposal Policy.</p>
<h3>10. Secure Storage and Disposal</h3>
<p>We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted clinical systems, role-based access controls, secure password protocols, regular backups, staff confidentiality training, confidential shredding of paper records, and secure deletion of electronic records.<br />
When retention periods expire, records are securely destroyed or permanently anonymised. We do not routinely notify individuals when records reach the end of their retention period and are securely destroyed.</p>
<h3>11. Your Rights</h3>
<p class="bottom-space-para">Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:</p>
<ul><li>Access your personal data (Subject Access Request)</li>
<li>Request correction of inaccurate data</li>
<li>Request restriction of processing</li>
<li>Object to processing (where applicable)</li>
<li>Request data portability (where applicable)</li>
<li>Withdraw consent for marketing at any time</li>
</ul>
<p>Medical records may not be erased where we are legally required to retain them.<br />
We will respond to Subject Access Requests within one calendar month unless a lawful extension applies.<br />
If you are dissatisfied with our handling of your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the <strong>Information Commissioner’s Office</strong> at (http://www.ico.org.uk).</p>
<h3>12. Automated Decision-Making</h3>
<p>We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.</p>
<h3>13. Changes to This Privacy Notice</h3>
<p>We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The latest version will always be available on our website.</p>
