    description: "As you get older, your body naturally produces less collagen and elastin, the proteins responsible for keeping your skin firm and flexible. This reduction can cause the skin to lose its elasticity and lead to wrinkles around the eyes."
  - title: "Facial Expressions"
    icon: "sparkle"
    description: "Every time you smile, frown, squint, or laugh, the muscles around your eyes contract. Over time, these repeated facial movements can create wrinkles in the delicate skin surrounding your eyes."
  - title: "Sun Exposure"
    icon: "eye"
    description: "Exposure to the sun's UV rays can damage the collagen and elastin in your skin. The skin becomes more prone to wrinkles as a result of this breakdown, and the area around your eyes is particularly susceptible to sun damage."
  - title: "Smoking"
    icon: "close"
    description: "Smoking speeds up the appearance of wrinkles by reducing oxygen flow to the skin and causing collagen and elastin to break down faster than usual."
  - title: "Genetics"
    icon: "compass"

  - title: "Hormonal Changes"
