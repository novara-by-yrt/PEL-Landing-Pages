  paragraphs:
    - "Ptosis eyes are also referred to as \"droopy eyelids.\" This means that your eyelids droop over your eyes. Ptosis can occur in adults due to muscle issues, or be present from birth (congenital ptosis) due to a levator muscle malfunction."
    - "Children may experience Marcus Gunn \"jaw-winking\" ptosis, where the eyelid lifts when the jaw opens, usually affecting one eyelid. Adults can develop ptosis later in life due to contact lens use, eye trauma, or cataract surgery, which can weaken the levator muscle tendon."
    - "If left untreated, ptosis can lead to astigmatism, blurred vision, and watery eyes due to the eyelid's weight pressing on the eye."
