<span>When was the last time you truly considered the health of your eyes? We blink, scroll, read, and stare, but do we ever stop and ask ourselves, "How can you take care of your eyes before problems begin?"</span>

<span>Your eyes are one of the most sensitive parts of your body. In today’s digital world, where screens dominate most of our time, it’s more important than ever to learn how to care for your eyes properly. From diet to lifestyle habits, there are simple things you can do every day to keep your eyes healthy and vision sharp.</span>

<span>Below, we’ll dive into the different </span><a href="/blog/how-to-keep-eyes-healthy"><b>ways to improve your eye health</b></a><span>, broken down into manageable tips for your everyday routine.</span>
<h2>Why Eye Care Is Important?</h2>
<span>We often take our vision for granted, only paying attention when something feels off. But regular care of the eyes helps prevent eye strain, infections, long-term damage, and even early signs of conditions like glaucoma or cataracts.</span>

<span>Sometimes symptoms like </span>sudden <a href="/blog/how-to-fix-droopy-eyelids-without-surgery"><strong>droopy eyelids</strong></a><span> can seem small but may signal underlying issues. This is why it's important to pay attention to changes and not ignore them.</span>
<h2>How to Take Care of the Eyes? - Essential Tips</h2>
<span>Here are some essential tips to help you protect and maintain your vision for the long run.</span>
<h3>1. Eat for Your Eyes</h3>
<span>What you put on your plate is essential for your eye health. Below are some of the main nutrients that will help you keep your eyes healthy.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Vitamin A helps</b><span> to maintain and prevent night-time vision problems.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Omega-3 fatty acids s</b><span>upport your eye moisture and lower the chance of macular degeneration.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Lutein &amp; Zeaxanthin are f</b><span>ound in leafy greens, and they help filter harmful blue light.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Vitamin C &amp; E help p</b><span>rotect the eyes from damage caused by free radicals.</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Tip:</b><span> Include foods like carrots, spinach, eggs, almonds, citrus fruits, and salmon fish in your daily meals.</span>
<h3>2. Protect Your Eyes from UV Rays</h3>
<span>Sunlight can seriously harm your eyes if you’re not careful. Overexposure to UV radiations can increase the risk of cataracts and macular degeneration.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Always wear UV-protective sunglasses, even on cloudy days.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>A wide-brimmed hat can offer extra protection.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Avoid direct and excessive sun exposure during peak hours (10 AM - 4 PM).</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>3. Limit Screen Time and Use the 20-20-20 Rule</h3>
<span>Screens are part of our everyday life, but so much screen time can cause  digital eye strain.</span>

<br /><br />
<b>What You Can Do:</b>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Adhere to the 20-20-20. That means you should look at something or any object 20 feet away from you for at least 20 seconds every 20 minutes.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Change your device screen brightness as per your environment.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Blink often to keep your eyes moist.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Use anti-glare screen protectors or blue light glasses if needed.</span></li>
</ul>
<span>Spending long hours on screens can also make your eyes feel overly watery. If you constantly deal with this issue, our guide on </span><a href="/blog/how-to-stop-watery-eyes"><b>how to stop watery eyes</b></a><span> breaks down the common causes and how to fix them.</span>
<h3>4. Keep Your Eyes Moisturised</h3>
<span>Dry eyes are becoming increasingly common due to air conditioning, screen exposure, and ageing.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Use a humidifier if your home has dry air.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Use artificial tears or lubricating eye drops.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Stay hydrated and drink enough water daily.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Avoid cigarette smoke and overly dry environments.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>5. Practice Good Eye Hygiene</h3>
<span>Poor hygiene can easily cause infections such as conjunctivitis as your eyes are the most sensitive part of your body. Here are the basic eye hygiene tips:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Do not touch or rub your eyes with dirty hands. </span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Remove makeup before going to sleep.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>If you use contact lenses, make sure to clean and keep them correctly.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Avoid sharing makeup or towels.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>6. Get Regular Eye Check-Ups</h3>
<span>Even if you see clearly, it’s still important to get your eyes checked regularly.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Eye exams can catch early signs of issues.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Children and adults should have an eye check-up at least once every 1-2 years.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>If you have chronic conditions like diabetes or high blood pressure, your eyes need even more attention.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>7. Quit Smoking</h3>
<span>Along with the lungs, smoking also affects your eyes. Risks of smoking include:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Higher chances of cataracts and optic nerve damage.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>It can worsen dry eye syndrome.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Raised risk of age-related macular degeneration.</span></li>
</ul>
<span>When you quit smoking, it greatly reduces these risks and supports better eye health.</span>
<h3>8. Use Proper Lighting While Reading or Working</h3>
<span>Do you put strain on your eyes in poor or dim light as can lead to fatigue and discomfort.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Use soft &amp; natural lighting.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Avoid glare and harsh overhead lighting.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Keep your screen at eye level to reduce strain on the neck and eyes.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>9. Give Your Eyes a Break</h3>
<span>Your eyes need a proper break like any other part of your body. Here are some simple and effective ways to relax your eyes.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Take frequent breaks from activities requiring focused attention, such as stitching or reading.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Rub your hands together to generate heat and gently place them over your closed eyes.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Close your eyes for a few minutes during the day to refresh them.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>10. Stay Active and Manage Health Conditions</h3>
<span>Your overall health affects your eyes more than you might realise.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Regular exercise improves blood circulation, which is great for your eyes.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Keep conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure, and cholesterol under control - they can lead to serious eye complications if unmanaged.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>11. Protect Your Eyes from Blue Light</h3>
<span>Spending long hours on your phone, computer, or tablet? You might experience eye strain, blurry vision, dry eyes, or even neck and shoulder discomfort. One major reason behind this is blue light - a high-energy light emitted by digital screens that our eyes aren’t built to block completely.</span>

<span>Excessive exposure over time may raise your chance of developing conditions, including cataracts and macular degeneration. Staring at screens for a long time can cause you to blink less, which can result in dry eye disease (DED). For contact lens wearers in particular, this results in pain, stinging, and blurred vision.</span>

<br /><br />
<strong>To protect your eyes:</strong>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Take regular screen breaks.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Use blue light filters or glasses.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Keep your screen at a comfortable distance.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Don’t forget to blink.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2>Final Thoughts</h2>
<span>Our eyes help us see, work, and enjoy the world around us. But with busy routines, long screen time, and exposure to dust or sunlight, we often ignore their care. Just like we look after our skin or body, our eyes also need regular attention to stay healthy.  </span>

<span>Whether it's eating the right food, protecting them from harmful light, or taking short breaks during screen use - small habits can make a big difference.</span>
