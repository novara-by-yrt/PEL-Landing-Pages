<span>A turkey neck is a term used to describe the sagging, wrinkled skin across the neck, which is said to be similar to the hanging wattle of a turkey. This condition usually becomes more evident with age as the skin loses its elasticity and the muscles become weaker. </span>

<span>Learning what it means to have a turkey neck and studying different methods to prevent or correct it will get people back to their youthful, firm necks again.</span>
<h2><b>What are the causes of Turkey Neck?</b></h2>
<span>There are various reasons why this condition occurs:</span>
<ol><li aria-level="1"><b>Ageing</b><span>: Collagen and elastin production diminishes with time, and there is a decrease in the firmness of the skin, thereby causing wrinkles and sagging skin.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Genetic</b><span>: Genetic susceptibility can cause skin to sag faster and at any age.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Exposure to Ultraviolet Rays:</b><span> Prolonged exposure to UV rays weakens the structural integrity of the skin, hastening the process of ageing, which contributes to sagging.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Weight fluctuations:</b><span> Severe weight loss or gain can stretch the skin, and repeated fluctuations may reduce its ability to retract, causing sagging.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Poor Posture: </b><span>Constantly looking down, such as when using smartphones or computers, can weaken neck muscles and contribute to skin sagging, a phenomenon sometimes referred to as "tech neck."</span></li>
</ol>

<h2><b>Turkey Neck Exercises</b></h2>
<span>There are certain exercises that can be added to your routine to strengthen neck muscles and improve skin tone. Though the effectiveness of these exercises is different for everyone, they are a non-invasive way to potentially reduce the appearance of a turkey neck.</span>
<h3><b>01. Forehead Push:</b></h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Put one hand on your forehead.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Push your head against your hand without allowing your head to move forward.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span> Maintain this for 10 seconds.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span> Then, take both your hands behind your head, push back with your neck, and hold for 10 seconds.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3><b>02. Mastication Exercise:</b></h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Sit upright</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Move your head backwards so that your chin is pointing to the ceiling.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Your lips are shut, and your mouth is to make chewing movements</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Do it 20 times.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3><b>03. Kissing the Ceiling:</b></h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span> Sit with your back straight.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span> Lift your head so that your chin points to the ceiling with your lips closed.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span> Pout your lips as if you were giving a kiss.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span> Repeat 20 times.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3><b>04. Neck Lift:</b></h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Lie on your bed with your head hanging over the edge and your face looking toward the ceiling.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Carefully and slowly lift your head up as high as you can, using your neck muscles.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Repeat five times.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Do not continue with this exercise if you feel your neck aches.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><b>Will Losing Weight Help Turkey Neck?</b></h2>
<span>Weight loss may vary the effects on a turkey neck. If excess fat causes sagging skin, losing weight will decrease the fullness under the chin. Sometimes, extreme weight loss may even worsen the looseness of the skin because the skin may not be elastic enough to fit the new contours. Additional treatments may then be needed to tighten the skin.</span>
<h2><b>Surgical Treatments for Turkey Neck Cure</b></h2>
<span>For people who want a more permanent solution, surgical procedures are the most effective way to eliminate turkey neck:</span>
<h3><b>01 Neck Lift (Plat ysmaplasty):</b></h3>
<span>This procedure tightens and removes excess skin from </span><span>the neck. The surgeon makes incisions behind each ear and possibly under the chin to access and tighten the underlying muscles, creating a smoother, more youthful appearance.</span>
<h3><b>02. Facelift (rhytidectomy): </b></h3>
<span>Often performed in conjunction with a neck lift, a facelift addresses sagging facial and neck muscles, removing excess skin to improve the overall contour of the neck and jawline. </span>
<h3><b>03. Liposuction</b><span>: </span></h3>
<span>For individuals with good skin elasticity but excess fat under the chin, liposuction can remove the fat deposits, resulting in a more defined neck and jawline. </span>
