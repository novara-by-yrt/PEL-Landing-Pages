<h2><strong>Under Eye Bags: What Are They and Why Do They Appear?
</strong></h2>
<span>Many people are concerned about under-eye bags, which are the most prevalent concern. Usually, this begins as a minor, sporadic swelling, but it might become severe, permanent, and broad with time. If left untreated, they may persist for a long time. Although eye bags are sometimes misinterpreted as an indication of tiredness, they result from a complex interplay of various factors such as heredity, lifestyle choices, and environment. </span>

<span>According to popular assumption, bags under the eyes are not a sign of exhaustion or sleep deprivation. Yet, these factors could make them look worse. </span>

<span>Understanding the causes is essential for finding the most effective treatment to minimize and remove bags under eyes:
</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Weakening Skin and Tissues: </b><span>As we age, the skin under our eyes weakens and thins. This allows fluid to gather and cause swelling. The loose skin can give rise to skin bags.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Loss of Muscle and internal tissue Support:</b><span> The muscles and internal tissues supporting our eyelids weaken with age. This allows fat that normally supports the eyes to prolapse and create a puffy appearance.</span></li>
</ul>
<span>However, the damage can be reduced by eliminating activities like smoking, alcohol consumption, sleep deprivation, and a diet high in salt. For optimal results, many people address eye bags with skincare products, over-the-counter medications, or under-eye fillers.</span>
<h2><strong>What causes bags under your eyes?</strong></h2>
<span>Are you frustrated by eye bags and endless searches for </span><b>how to treat eye bags?</b><span> But hold on, have you gotten to the root cause yet? Before exploring the various treatments, it's critical to determine what causes eye bags. There are several reasons why under eye bags develop. Below is a breakdown of some common causes.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>With age, the breakdown of the fatty tissue in the upper cheeks causes bulges to protrude below the eyes.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>The orbital septum, a thin membrane, presses against the skin beneath the eyes to retain fat. If it weakens, the fat may sag, resulting in under-eye bags.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Smoking can dehydrate the skin, and medical professionals believe that quitting smoking at night might worsen eye puffing by interfering with sleep.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>While genetics can contribute significantly to the appearance of under eye bags, it is not always the main cause.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Consuming alcohol can dry the skin, increasing the likelihood that it will sink under the eyes.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Lack of sleep can widen blood vessels, which can result in eye bags under the eyes or make them worse.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>A diet high in sodium can affect the delicate area around the eyes, increasing the appearance of bags and promoting water retention.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Nasal congestion brought on by allergies and irritations can result in blood pools developing under the eyes, which can produce puffiness around the eyes.
</span></li>
</ul>

<span>Under eye bags are one of the most prevalent concerns people have about their appearance, as they make them look fatigued. There are various surgical and non-surgical treatments available to remove eye bags, so it's wise to think about your options. Below are some of the eye bag treatments. </span>
<h3 class="non-tab-blog"><b>Surgical Professional Treatments to Get Rid of Bags Under Eyes</b></h3>
<h3><b>Eyelid surgery</b></h3>
<span>If you have bags under your eyes, there are treatment options available, one of which is <a href="/surgical/eyelid-surgery/eye-bag-removal-blepharoplasty-uk">eye bag removal</a> surgery, also known as blepharoplasty. The process aims to eliminate or relocate extra skin and fat in the lower eyelid area. The extent of skin and fat removal or modification depends upon the severity of your eyebags, the condition of your skin and tissues, and the shape of your overall face. Excess fat is removed through an incision in the natural crease inside the lower lid. The procedure is typically performed in an outpatient setting and requires local anaesthesia.</span>

<span>After the procedure, there is usually considerable swelling and bruising, which may last for 2 to 3 weeks. Before deciding on eye bag removal to </span><b>remove bags under eyes</b><span>, it is essential to consult with a competent and experienced oculoplastic surgeon to discuss your options in detail.</span>

<span>Blepharoplasty can also address the following issues:</span>

<span>- Baggy or puffy upper eyelids.</span>

<span>- Excess skin of the upper eyelid that obstructs vision.</span>

<span>- Droopy lower eyelids.</span>

<span>- Excess skin on lower eyelids.</span>

<span>It is important to discuss the potential side effects of eyelid surgery with your healthcare provider, which may include dry eyes, pain, watery eyes, swelling, bruising, and blurred vision. However, rare complications may include visual loss, bleeding, infection, injury to eye muscles, corneal abrasion, and drooping of an eyelid.</span>

<h2><b>Risk Factors Associated with Bags Under Eyes
</b></h2>
<span>Eye bags are generally not a cause for concern, but they can sometimes indicate other underlying health issues. Below are some of them:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Sudden Appearance:</b><span> If your eye bags appear suddenly, it could be a sign of a thyroid condition.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Allergies:</b><span> Seasonal and other allergies can trigger histamines that your body releases that lead to fluid leakage from blood vessels and puffiness under the eyes.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Eye Infection:</b><span> An infection such as pink eye or inflammation resulting from dry eye syndrome can also cause skin swelling. If you experience these symptoms, it is advisable to consult a doctor to receive antibiotics or eye drops to treat the underlying condition.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Systemic Diseases:</b><span> Conditions such as kidney failure can also contribute to the development of eye bags.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Smoking:</b><span> Smoking habits can exacerbate the problem, as it worsens sleep quality, decreases collagen production, and reduces skin elasticity.
</span></li>
</ul>
