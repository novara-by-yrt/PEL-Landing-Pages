<span>Under-eye wrinkles and fine lines are common in many people. They are harmless and just a normal part of ageing as the skin's capacity to regenerate itself decreases with age. You may be wondering how to eliminate fine lines under your eyes. If so, we've got you covered! <h2>Causes of Wrinkles Under the Eyes</h2>
<span>Under eye wrinkles are a common concern as we age. The skin around the eyes is thin and has less elasticity, which makes it easier to form wrinkles and fine lines. The following are some of the common causes of wrinkles under eyes that can help in taking preventive measures and choosing appropriate treatments.</span>

<strong>Causes:</strong>

<span>- Thinning of the skin around the eyes</span>

<span>- Loss of elasticity</span>

<span>- Frequent sun exposure</span>

<span>- Smoking</span>

<span>- Repeated facial movements</span>

<span>- Genetic traits</span>

<span>- Depleted levels of collagen in the dermis layer of skin</span>

<span>- Environmental factors</span>
<h2>How to Eliminate Fine Lines Under Eyes? - Medical Treatments</h2>
<span>Fine lines and wrinkles are among the earliest indications of ageing that frequently appear in the delicate skin under the eyes. Several factors, such as ageing naturally, sun exposure, dehydration, repeated facial movements, and lifestyle choices, might result in these lines. This part of the skin is more vulnerable to these early indications of ageing because it is thinner and lacks the oil glands that naturally moisturise the skin. A mix of healthy skincare habits, lifestyle adjustments, and occasionally expert treatments are used to eliminate fine lines under eyes. The following are a few of the most appreciated treatments to think about:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3>Wrinkle relaxing injections</h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Quick and widely used, injectable under eye wrinkle treatment minimises fine lines and wrinkles without the need for surgery. A tiny amount of a neurotoxin will be injected into the skin by your practitioner during an under-eye wrinkle treatment to enhance the look of your eye area, by encouraging relaxation of dynamic and static wrinkles, and provide a more youthful look.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3>Tear trough fillers</h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Tear trough dermal fillers are collagen-boosting and revitalising that significantly eliminate wrinkles, dark circles, and hollowness under the eyes. Through the stimulation of collagen formation, it works to counteract the signs of ageing and leave the area around your eyes glowing again.</span>

<span>Dermal fillers can be used to reshape, tighten, lift, and add volume to the area under eyes. Different types of dermal fillers may be more effective in treating various skin concerns.</span>

<span>This treatment can successfully treat any gaunt, darkish under eye area, revive the area around the eyes, and soften fine lines. Its main benefits are plumping and volume restoration. Teosyal Redensity2,  a brand specially designed for smooth, natural results, is used in all tear trough dermal filler procedures.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3>Polynucleotides</h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>A rejuvenating anti-ageing treatment that aids in fibroblast longevity and restoration of healthy skin. With polynucleotides (trout or salmon DNA in saline or hyaluronic acid), you can fight against ageing and restore any hyaluronic acid that you may have lost over time.</span>

<span>It works to treat the entire face, giving your complexion a natural "lift." It also helps to tighten the skin around the eyes, and smooth out smile lines and creases in the cheeks and crow's feet area.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3>Microneedling</h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Microneedling also known as collagen-inducing treatment, this approach entails creating microscopic punctures in the skin to encourage the regeneration of skin cells and trigger the body's natural healing mechanism.</span>

<span>Two proteins that are essential for preventing wrinkles, collagen and elastin, are created when this response is triggered, giving the appearance of being younger and more radiant straight away. Additionally, this treatment works well to balance out pigmentation, reduce scarring, and minimise UV radiation. Treating several issues with a single treatment would be an excellent use of this tool.</span>

<span>Chemical peels help enhance skin tone and brighten tired and lifeless skin. They also lessen the visibility of static wrinkles and fine lines. One peel will give you noticeable effects straight away, making your skin look younger, softer, and brighter.</span>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<b>1. Does Vaseline remove fine lines and wrinkles around eyes?</b>

<span>Vaseline can have some of the similar hydrating benefits as your favourite eye cream, especially when used with a moisturising cream. Unfortunately, Vaseline won't do much to help remove deeply set fine lines or brighten dark circles.</span>

<b>2. Does coconut oil make skin less wrinkled?</b>

<span>Coconut oil and other natural treatments have not shown to be any more successful than standard over-the-counter treatments. These may help minimise the look of mild to moderate wrinkles, but deeper lines would probably require more active treatments.</span>

<b>3. Are wrinkles and fine lines under eyes permanent?</b>

<span>Unfortunately, touching the area around the eyes results in noticeable wrinkles because the skin is so thin there. Even if a few strong "rubbing sessions" won't result in permanent under-eye wrinkles, these effects do build over time and may eventually lead to permanent wrinkling.</span>
