<span>Have you ever looked in the mirror and noticed that the skin around your eyebrows seems to be casting a shadow over your beautiful eyes? </span><span>If yes, this condition is commonly known as hooded eyes. Before exploring treatments, it is important to understand what are </span><a href="/blog/hooded-eyes-causes-and-symptoms"><b>hooded eyes</b></a><span> and its causes &amp; symptoms first.</span>

<span>Whether your goal is a more drastic or subtle transformation, you'll get all the information you need about hooded eyelids before get to know about <strong><a href="/blog/how-to-get-rid-of-hooded-eyes">how to get rid of hooded eyes</a></strong> the treatment here. So, get ready to appreciate the beauty of your hooded eyes and realise the full potential of your enticing glance.</span>
<h2><b>How to Get Rid of Hooded Eyes? </b></h2>
<span>Many people are concerned about hooded eyes since they can make their eyes look smaller and give the appearance of being sleepy or old. Fortunately, hooded eyes can be treated and the overall appearance of the eyes improves with multiple treatments. Let's look at the following:</span>
<h2><b>Surgical Treatments for Hooded Eyelids</b></h2>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><strong>Upper lid Blepharoplasty</strong></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<a href="/surgical/eyelid-surgery/upper-eyelid-blepharoplasty-uk"><span>Upper Blepharoplasty</span></a><span> or Upper Eyelid Lift Surgery is the best long-term treatment for hooded eyes. The excess drooping skin and tissue from the upper eyelids are removed during an upper blepharoplasty.</span>

<span>Your upper eyelid crease is gently incised by the surgeon. Subsequently, any extra skin and fat that's pulling down your eyes is cut off. The levator muscle, which regulates your top eyelid, is then tightened, which aids in raising your eyelid into a more aesthetically acceptable posture. Finally, the incision is closed using careful stitching methods intended to minimise visible scars.</span>

<span>Upper blepharoplasty is an easy operation with a quick recovery. After your recovery, you should be able to see more clearly without having to constantly strain.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><strong>Brow Lift</strong></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>A <a href="/surgical/browlift-treatment-uk">brow lift</a> is a cosmetic operation used to rejuvenate the eyes and raise the eyebrows. It is sometimes referred to as an upper facelift or forehead lift.</span>

<span>By strategically relaxing the muscles surrounding the eyes using injections, the appearance of hooding decreases and the eyebrows are raised. These injections frequently give the impression of something smoother and more youthful.</span>

<span>There are various methods for performing brow lift surgery: One technique is called a direct brow lift, and it entails making a single, long cut that runs from just above the eye brows. Another one known as a limited incision or pre-trichial temporal brow lift, makes two smaller incisions, one above each temple in the hairline. As an alternative, endoscopic brow lifts are performed through very tiny incisions made behind the hairline. Other brow lift procedures include Mid-forehead brow lifts which is an option for people with receding hairlines and deep forehead wrinkles.</span>
<h3></span><b>Benefits of Using Surgical Treatment for Hooded Eyelids</b></h3>
<span>While non-surgical procedures offer a gentler approach, surgical treatments for hooded eyes can provide distinct benefits.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>More Dramatic and Long-Lasting Results: </b><span>Surgery directly addresses the root cause of hooded eyes - excess skin and fat. This can lead to a more drastic and long-lasting improvement in appearance that may take several years to appear.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Improved Vision:</b><span> In severe cases of hooded eyes, drooping skin can obstruct the upper field of vision. Blepharoplasty can significantly enhance peripheral vision by removing this obstruction.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Greater Precision:</b><span> Surgery enables a more targeted approach to address specific concerns. The surgeon can precisely remove excess skin and fat while preserving natural contours and folds.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Addressing Underlying Muscle Issues:</b><span> Weak muscles can cause hooded eyes. Blepharoplasty can address these muscle issues and remove excess tissue.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><span><b>Non-Surgical Treatments for Hooded Eyelids</b></h2>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Dermal Fillers</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Dermal fillers, such as hyaluronic acid-based fillers, can be strategically injected around the brow and eye area to lift and sculpt the skin, reducing the appearance of hooded eyelids. The fillers work by adding volume to targeted areas, which can help smooth out wrinkles and restore a more youthful contour to the eyes.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Laser therapy</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Laser therapy can lessen the appearance of hooded eyelids. It targets the area around the eyelids with specific attention to tightening and firming the skin. The collagen-producing heat from the laser can result in tighter, more youthful-looking skin around the eyes.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Thread lifts</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Thread lifts involve dissolvable threads to lift and realign sagging or hooded skin around the eyelids. This minimally surgical technique can give the eyes a small but visible lift, which can help them appear brighter and more youthful.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Topical Treatments</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>A few topical treatments, like tightening serums and lotions, might help to improve the look of hooded eyelids. These creams can help tighten and moisturise the skin, which might temporarily lessen hooding, however, their effects are not as noticeable as those of other treatments.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Sofwave Treatment</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>The Sofwave procedure is an advanced method that produces collagen deep into your skin using ultrasonic radiation without requiring needles or incisions. The skin around the eyes is gently tightened with this technique, giving the area a more lifted and young appearance. Because Sofwave is FDA-approved and needs minimal downtime, you can get back to your regular activities right away.</span>
<h3></span><b>Benefits of Using Non Surgical Treatment for Hooded Eyelids</b></h3>
<span>Considering non surgical treatment for hooded eyelids over traditional ones has several benefits. Here are some of the advantages:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Less invasive:</b><span> Non-surgical procedures do not involve any cutting or stitching, minimising risks and discomfort.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Shorter recovery time</b><span>: Non-surgical treatments typically involve minimal downtime due to the absence of incisions. Patients can usually resume normal activities right away or within a day or two.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>More affordable:</b><span> Non-surgical treatments are usually more affordable than blepharoplasty.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>More natural-looking results:</b><span> Non-surgical procedures can subtly enhance the appearance of hooded eyes without creating a dramatically different look.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Fewer risks:</b><span> Because non-surgical treatments are less invasive, they carry a lower risk of complications compared to surgery.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>How to fix hooded eyes naturally</strong></h2>
<span>Here are a few natural approaches that could help give your eyes a slight lift:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Cooling Compresses:</b><span> The anti-inflammatory properties of cucumbers can help reduce puffiness, a common contributor to hooded eye appearance. Chilled cucumber slices applied for 15-20 minutes can provide temporary relief.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Strategic Makeup Application:</b><span> Opting for lighter eyeshadow shades on the lid and avoiding heavy eyeliner on the upper lash line can create the illusion of a more open eye.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Soothing Chamomile:</b><span> Chamomile tea bags boast natural anti-inflammatory properties. Steeping them in hot water, letting them cool, and then applying them gently to closed eyelids for 10-15 minutes may help reduce puffiness and soothe the delicate eye area.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Eyelid Exercises:</b><span> Regularly performing targeted eye exercises may help strengthen the muscles around the eyes, potentially leading to a subtle reduction in hooding over time. Consulting a qualified professional for guidance on proper technique is recommended.</span></li>
</ul>
<span>While these approaches can offer temporary relief and potentially minimise puffiness, they are unlikely to produce long-lasting, dramatic changes in hooded eye appearance. </span>
<h2><strong>Conclusion</strong></h2>
<span>The optimal treatment method for hooded eyes depends on your preferences and goals. There is a modest way to improve the appearance of hooded eyes without resorting to surgery. However, surgical methods, like <strong><a href="/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-blepharoplasty">blepharoplasty</a></strong>, can give more striking and durable results. It's essential to consult with a skilled plastic surgeon to figure out the best course of action for getting the desired appearance. </span>

<span>Whichever technique is used, it's crucial to keep in mind that hooded eyes can be a really attractive characteristic. With the right knowledge and techniques, you can improve your appeal and feel good about your eyes.</span>
