<span>Droopy eyelids are one of those unmistakable signs of ageing that make us look tired or downcast, especially when paired with eye bags and dark circles. As we age, the skin on our upper eyelids can become looser, and the facial muscles may weaken. The skin loses its elasticity, resulting in more noticeable sagging around the eyes. In some cases of mild visual obstruction, droopy eyelid workout can be beneficial.</span>

<span>While plastic surgery is often the go-to solution for severe eyelid drooping (especially when it starts to affect vision), there are some exercises you can try that might help improve the appearance of droopy eyelids. If done regularly, exercises that target the muscles around the eyes can potentially make a difference.</span>

<span>Let's check out all the eyelid workouts that can help strengthen the muscles around your eyes.</span>
<h2>Top 9 Eyelid Strengthening Exercises</h2>
<span>If you're starting to notice that your eyes are looking fatigued or that your eyelids are feeling heavier, certain eyelid exercises can help. These eyelid muscle exercises are safe and easy to do at home, and incorporating them into your routine may support eyelid firmness and elasticity.</span>

<span>Face exercises have gained popularity recently, as many people have reported seeing improvements in their eyelids and sagging skin. Participants often shared that they experienced noticeable enhancements in various facial areas and felt highly satisfied with the results. To experience any benefits, it's generally recommended to commit to regular practice - around 20 to 30 minutes a day, six to seven days a week.</span>
<h3>1. Warming Up</h3>
<span>It's essential to warm up the eyelid area. Even if you don't exercise, cleaning, warming up, and lightly massaging your eyelids has been shown to improve circulation and nerve responses. One fast method to achieve this is to softly rub your palms together to generate heat, and then position your warm hands over your closed eyes. Hold for a few seconds, allowing the heat to relax the eye muscles and prepare them for the workout. This can also improve blood circulation and reduce puffiness around the eyes.</span>
<h3>2. Blinking Exercises</h3>
<span>Blinking sounds simple, but it’s an effective way to activate the muscles around your eyes. For this exercise:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Close your eyes for a few seconds.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Then, quickly open them wide and hold for a couple of seconds.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Repeat this 10-15 times.</span></li>
</ul>
<span>This can improve eyelid strength, especially if done several times throughout the day.</span>
<h3>3. Eyelid Lifts</h3>
<span>Eyelid lifts are specifically targeted to lift the upper eyelid area.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Place a finger gently under your eyebrows and lift them slightly.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>As you lift, slowly close your eyes and feel the stretch in your upper lids.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Hold this position for five seconds and then release.</span></li>
</ul>
<span>Repeat this exercise 10 times daily. Over time, this sagging eyelids exercise may help with upper eyelid firmness and reduce sagging.</span>
<h3>4. Eye Squeeze and Hold</h3>
<span>This exercise involves holding a squint to strengthen eyelid muscles:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Gently close your eyes as if squinting but not fully shutting them.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Hold the squint for five seconds, then release.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Repeat this for about 10 repetitions. This routine targets both the lower and upper eyelids, building endurance in the muscles.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>5. Basic Muscle Stimulation</h3>
<span>Direct stimulation may help reduce </span><a href="/condition/droopy-ptosis-eye"><b>droopy eyelids</b></a><span>. You can do this by moving your eyes or using your fingertips. For this exercise:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Use your fingertips to gently press down on your brow bone while trying to lift your eyelids.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Maintain the pressure for several seconds and then let go.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Repeat this move around 10 times. This resistance-based movement can help strengthen the eyelids against gentle pressure, which potentially reduces droop over time.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>6. Eye Patch Workout</h3>
<span>Using an eye patch is one of the most commonly recommended treatments for a lazy eye or amblyopia because it’s effective, safe, and budget-friendly. Wearing an eye patch over the stronger eye encourages the weaker eye to work harder, which helps improve its communication with the brain. Wearing an eye patch can also strengthen eye muscles and promote balanced vision. However, it’s wise to consult an eye specialist before trying this to ensure it’s suitable for your specific case.</span>
<h3>7. Trataka Yogic Eye Exercise</h3>
<span>Trataka, a yoga practice known for enhancing eye health, includes a specific exercise called Bahiranga. Here’s how to perform it in four simple steps:</span>

<br /><br />
<b>Sit Comfortably: </b><span>Begin by sitting upright in a meditative position, either on the floor or on a firm chair. Maintain a straight posture and allow your hands to relax with palms facing down.</span>

<br /><br />
<b>Focus Forward:</b><span> Look straight ahead at one steady point with your eyes open.</span>

<br /><br />
<b>Shift Your Focus:</b><span> Slowly look towards your right shoulder without turning your head. Try to keep your eyes focused there for a few seconds and avoid blinking if you can.</span>

<br /><br />
<b>Return to Centre and Repeat:</b><span> Bring your eyes back to the centre, then repeat the same movement to the left side.</span>
<h3>8. Eye Rolls</h3>
<span>Eye rolls are simple yet effective for keeping eye muscles flexible:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Look up, then slowly move your eye in a full circle clockwise.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>After one circle, roll your eyes in the opposite direction.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Repeat five times in each direction.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Eye rolls can help reduce stiffness around the eyes, promote circulation, and keep the surrounding muscles active.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>9. Diagonal Stretch</h3>
<span>The diagonal stretch exercise is simple and can help improve eye flexibility and coordination. Here's how to do it:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Position Your Fingers: Gently place a fingertip at the outer end of each eyebrow, facing forward.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Look Downward: Using your eye muscles, slowly look downward as far as you comfortably can.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Move Eyes Side to Side: Let your eyes glide from one side to the other, similar to a pendulum's motion.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Repeat: Continue this movement for approximately 20 seconds to finish the exercise.</span></li>
</ul>
