<span>Dermal fillers, sometimes known as "<a href="/non-surgical/tear-trough-fillers-uk">tear trough filler</a>" or "under eye filler," are a well-liked, non-invasive procedure that can be used on the cheeks, lips, and under the eyes. This transitory treatment is an effective alternative to surgical procedures &amp; it is becoming more widespread in mainstream society.</span>

<span>Tear trough fillers are designed to reduce signs of ageing, such as eye bags, hollowness &amp; dark circles under the eyes. They can also improve tear troughs and add volume beneath the skin. A low volume of hyaluronic acid is injected into the hollowed area to create a smoother, more youthful appearance.</span>

<span>This blog will help you learn <a href="/blog/12223-2">how long does tear trough filler last</a> &amp; what you can do to maintain the results. Read on to know more.</span>
<h2>How Long Does Tear Trough Filler Last?</h2>
<span>Hyaluronic acid is highly concentrated in tear trough filler injections. This substance is naturally present in the body, providing skin hydration and volume. Tear trough fillers last from 6 months to a few years with minimal downtime. These fillers gradually degrade over time, which requires touch-ups to maintain results. </span>

<span>Most people are able to maintain their results for six to eighteen months. The duration of a tear trough treatment, however, differs from person to person. Numerous factors, including your age, skin type, injection techniques, and filler type &amp; placement, influence this.</span>

<span>Let's look at the factors that determine how long your tear trough filler will last:</span>
<ul><li>
<h4><strong>Age</strong></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Younger patients typically have longer-lasting results and better aesthetic results as their skin is thicker and tighter or more resilient, so it holds the hyaluronic acid gel well.</span>
<ul><li>
<h4><strong>Quality of Skin</strong></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Everybody's skin type is unique; thicker, tighter skin holds fillers well, whilst thinner under-eye skin does not hold the filler. The more favourable your skin was before getting fillers, the more likely your tear trough treatments will provide longer results.</span>
<ul><li>
<h4><strong>Technique of Injection</strong></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>The short and long-term outcomes of the treatment can be greatly influenced by the expertise and experience of your injector. For best effects, a skilled practitioner will know exactly where to apply filler and how much to use, resulting in an improvement that looks natural and lasts as long as feasible.</span>

<span>Additionally, they might lessen side effects like swelling or bruises, which might cause your fillers to lose their effectiveness sooner. In this area, either a needle or a cannula can be used; however, using a cannula is generally thought to be safer and to lower the chance of problems.</span>
<h3>Types of Tear Trough Fillers</h3>
<span>There are long-lasting fillers that have an indefinite duration. Permanent fillers such as silicone, acrylic, and calcium are not advisable for use under the eyes.</span>

<span>Most under eye fillers in use today are composed of hyaluronic acid, a chain of sugar molecules. They vary in hyaluronic acid concentrations, particle sizes, and cross-linking, affecting their longevity. It is not advisable to place fillers that should be injected in the cheek under the eye.</span>
<h3>The Placement of the Tear Trough Filler</h3>
<span>All hyaluronic fillers remain in the body for over a year, and in some cases, they can last for 5-10 years. As time passes, the fillers break down gradually because our bodies produce an enzyme called hyaluronidase, which breaks the bonds of the sugar chains in the gel. As the volume of filler decreases, the effect diminishes.</span>

<span>However, filler placed near the surface of the skin will be more visible for a longer period. This is not necessarily advantageous, as shallow filler can appear bluish or lumpy. Although deeper filler may seem to vanish more quickly, it is simply concealed by more of your tissue, similar to a thicker blanket.</span>

<span>Deep filler tends to appear more natural, but you might perceive that when half of the filler has been absorbed by your body, it seems as though all of it is gone. The key point is that the technique used impacts how your filler appears and how long it appears good.</span>
<h3>The Amount of Filler Used Under the Eyes </h3>
<span>A small amount of filler can help reduce or eliminate under-eye shadows, but it can also appear to fade in less than a year. In cases with severe cheek deflation due to ageing which can contribute to deep under-eye creases, filler placed in the cheeks may be beneficial.</span>

<span>Furthermore, filler may appear to last longer than a year. Too much filler can sometimes give the area under the eyes an unappealing appearance. It has the ability to change the natural slopes and contours. If it happens to you, it is advisable that the filler not last for many years. In fact, you might require an injection of the enzyme hyaluronidase to reverse the filler.</span>
