<h2><strong>Polynucleotides and Why Aftercare Matters</strong></h2>
<strong><a href="/non-surgical/polynucleotide-treatment-uk">Polynucleotides Under Eye</a></strong> are naturally occurring molecules that promote cell regeneration and tissue repair. When injected into the skin, they stimulate fibroblasts, enhancing collagen production and skin hydration. This makes them highly effective for addressing wrinkles, fine lines, acne scars, and overall skin ageing.

Still, as with any aesthetic procedure, the efficacy of polynucleotide aftercare will ultimately yield the most optimal results. Skipping post-procedure care may result in longer-lasting redness, swelling, or compromised healing. Following professional guidelines on polynucleotide treatment aftercare can help accelerate the healing process and leave you glowing healthy-looking skin.
<h3><strong>Immediate Polynucleotides Aftercare: The First 24 Hours</strong></h3>
The first 24 hours after your treatment are crucial. Follow these key steps:
<h4>1. Avoid Touching or Rubbing the Treated Area</h4>
<ul><li>Refuse to touch or rub your skin after getting your polynucleotide injections.</li>
 	<li>Touching the area transfers bacteria, which raises the infection risk.</li>
</ul>
<h4>2. Clean Your Skin, but Not with Harsh Products</h4>
<ul><li>Wash your face with a gentle cleanser and lukewarm water.</li>
 	<li>Steer clear of exfoliants, harsh acids, or retinoids for at least 48 hours.</li>
</ul>
<h4>3. Reduce Sun Exposure</h4>
<ul><li>Direct sunlight may lead to irritation and hyperpigmentation.</li>
 	<li>If you have to venture outdoors, use a broad-spectrum SPF 50 sunscreen.</li>
</ul>
<h4>4. Avoid Makeup for 24 Hours</h4>
<ul><li>Allow your skin to breathe and heal naturally.</li>
 	<li>If needed, use only mineral-based makeup after the initial healing process.</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Polynucleotides Treatment Aftercare: The First Week</strong></h3>
As your skin heals further, take these vital polynucleotides treatment aftercare tips to achieve the best results:
<h4>1. Drink Ample Water</h4>
<ul><li>Drinking lots of water keeps the skin hydrated and increases the effects of polynucleotides.</li>
</ul>
<h4>2. Use a Soothing Moisturizer</h4>
<ul><li>Use fragrance-free, hypoallergenic moisturisers to keep your skin moisturised.</li>
</ul>
<h4>3. Shun Heat and Excessive Perspiration</h4>
<ul><li>Avoid hot showers, saunas, and steam rooms for at least 72 hours.</li>
 	<li>Sweating may irritate, so avoid heavy exercise (refer to our polynucleotides aftercare exercise section below).</li>
</ul>
<h4>4. Sleep with Your Head Elevated</h4>
<ul><li>This can help minimise swelling and improve circulation.</li>
</ul>
<h4>5. Avoid Alcohol and Smoking</h4>
<ul><li>Both can delay healing and undermine your results.</li>
</ul>

<h2><strong>Polynucleotides Aftercare Exercise: What You Ought to Know</strong></h2>
The typical question many patients ask is, "May I exercise following my treatment?" Here is everything you need to know about polynucleotides aftercare exercise:
<h3><strong>1. Stay away from vigorous exercise for 48-72 hours</strong></h3>
Severe exercises raise blood flow and may result in swelling or bruising.
<h3><strong>2. Go for Light Movement</strong></h3>
Walking or stretching lightly is acceptable, but avoid activities that result in profuse sweating.
<h3><strong>3. Stay Cool</strong></h3>
Excessive heat can make the treated area sensitive, so avoid hot yoga or prolonged sun exposure.
<h3><strong>4. Ease Back into Your Routine</strong></h3>
At 3-4 days, you can gradually return to moderate exercises.
