<h2><b>What are Polynucleotides?</b></h2>
<span>Polynucleotides consist of chains of DNA and RNA fragments obtained from salmon or trout sperm, processed and engineered for regenerative medicine. <a href="/non-surgical/polynucleotide-treatment-uk/"><strong>Polynucleotides</strong></a> are bio-stimulating compounds that stimulate the activity of fibroblasts, tissue regeneration, and collagen production. They not only beautify ageing skin but also trigger lasting healing and anti-inflammatory action.</span>

<span>Injections with polynucleotides are of special advantage when it comes to:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Deepest skin moisturiser</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Stimulation of collagen</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Texturing and tone-enhancing</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Reducing redness and inflammation</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><b>What is Profhilo</b></h2>
<span>Profhilo is a stabilised version of hyaluronic acid (HA), created not to increase volume but to deeply hydrate and enhance skin elasticity. In contrast to regular fillers, <strong>Profhilo</strong> diffuses underneath the skin to give a dewy, plump appearance without changing facial contours.</span>

<span>Profhilo is most effective for:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Fine lines and wrinkles</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Loss of skin elasticity</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Mild to moderate sagging</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>General skin laxity</span></li>
</ul>
<span>Its single, dual action of stimulating four forms of collagen and elastin makes it a treatment of choice for many seeking a noninvasive anti-ageing remedy.</span>
<h2><b>Treatment Process: Polynucleotide Treatment vs Profhilo</b></h2>
<span>The process of application for both treatments is quite similar, but has distinct nuances:</span>
<h3><b>Polynucleotides</b><span>:</span></h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Typically done in 3 sessions, 2-4 weeks apart</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Can be done even on the under-eye regions and sensitive skin</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Often done with microneedling or radiofrequency for better results</span></li>
</ul>
<h3><b>Profhilo</b><span>:</span></h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Usually two sessions, 4 weeks apart</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Involves the BAP (Bio Aesthetic Points) method—5 injection points on each side of the face</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Very little downtime and extremely natural results</span></li>
</ul>
<span>When compared to polynucleotide treatment, Profhilo requires more sessions but provides regenerative healing, whereas the former targets instant hydration and glow.</span>

<span>Polynucleotides regenerate and repair the skin at the cellular level, while Profhilo gives deep hydration and stimulates collagen for a plumping, radiant effect.</span>

<span>Profhilo usually requires 2 sessions 4 weeks apart. Polynucleotides usually require 3 to 4 sessions 2–3 weeks apart for best results.</span>
