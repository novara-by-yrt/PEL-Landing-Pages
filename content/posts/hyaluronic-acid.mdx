<h2><strong><span><span>What is Hyaluronic Acid?</span></span></strong></h2>
<span><span>Hyaluronic acid is a naturally occurring, slippery, gel-like substance found throughout the body, especially in the skin, joints, &amp; eyes. Functioning as an anti-aging component in skin care products, it helps to retain moisture in the skin.</span></span>

<span><span>Hyaluronic acid is the skin's primary source of water. It's one of the greatest ways to address dryness and firmness loss while also protecting the fundamental structures of the skin. Since hyaluronic acid serves a number of roles in maintaining hydrated, plump, smooth, and soft skin, it actually gained huge popularity very quickly. It plays several important roles in keeping your body functioning smoothly.</span></span>
