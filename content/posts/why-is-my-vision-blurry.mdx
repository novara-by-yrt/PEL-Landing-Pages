<span>Do you wonder, ‘Why are my eyes blurry?’ Blurry vision is usually caused by refractive errors like short-sightedness, long-sightedness, or astigmatism,  any lifestyle factors or medical conditions like high blood pressure. </span>

<span>In most cases, it is a simple fix. It might mean you need new glasses. It might mean your eyes are dry. But in rare cases, it is a warning sign from your body. It is important to know the difference. </span>

<span>This guide explains why vision becomes blurry and how to address it safely.</span>
<h2><b>Why are My Eyes Blurry? Causes Explained</b></h2>
<span>Blurred vision can be caused by various reasons; here’s the breakdown:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Blurred Vision Due to Refractive Errors</b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>If blurriness develops gradually, it is likely a refractive error. This means the eye cannot focus light correctly. It is very common. It is usually easily fixed with glasses or contact lenses.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Myopia (Short-sightedness)</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>You can see things close up perfectly. You can read a text message on your phone. But distant objects look fuzzy. The TV screen is blurry. Road signs are hard to read. This happens when the eye focuses light in front of the retina instead of on it.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Hyperopia (Long-sightedness)</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>You can see things far away clearly. But close objects look blurry. Reading a book is difficult. The dashboard in the car looks fuzzy. This happens when the eye focuses light behind the retina.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Astigmatism</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>This is a shape problem. A normal eye is round like a football. An eye with astigmatism is shaped like a rugby ball or an egg. This bends light unevenly. Vision is distorted at all distances. Lights at night might look streaky or like starbursts.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Presbyopia (The ‘Over 40' Shift)</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>This happens to everyone. It typically starts between ages 40 and 45. The lens inside the eye gets harder. It loses flexibility. The eye muscles cannot squeeze the lens to focus up close. You might find yourself holding menus or phones further away to read them. This is not a disease. It is a natural part of ageing.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Medical Conditions That May Cause Blurry Vision</b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Your eyes are connected to the rest of your body. Systemic health issues often show up in your vision first.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Diabetes (High Blood Sugar)</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>High sugar levels affect the whole body. They cause the lens of the eye to swell with fluid. This changes the shape of the lens. Vision becomes blurry when sugar is high. It often resolves when blood sugar levels return to normal. Important: Long-term high sugar can damage blood vessels in the retina. This is called Diabetic Retinopathy, which may lead to permanent vision loss if not treated.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>High Blood Pressure</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Hypertension is a silent killer. It damages the tiny blood vessels in the back of the eye. This is called Hypertensive Retinopathy. It can cause the vessels to narrow, leak, or burst. Usually, there are no symptoms until it is advanced. Sudden blurred vision in these patients needs a doctor's check.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Cataracts</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Inside your eye, there is a clear lens. As we age, proteins build up on this lens. It becomes cloudy. It is like looking through a dirty window or through fog. Colours might look dull. Night driving becomes difficult because of glare. This is very common in older adults.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Lifestyle Factors Causing Blurred Eyesight</b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Sometimes, your eyes are healthy, but your habits are causing problems. Modern life is hard on our vision.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Digital Eye Strain</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>We stare at computers and phones for hours. This is called ‘</span><b>Computer Vision Syndrome</b><span>’. When looking at screens, we blink less often. Blinking spreads tears and keeps vision clear. Without blinking, the eye surface gets dry and rough. </span>

<span>Symptoms include:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="2"><span>Blurry vision after work.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="2"><span>Headaches.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="2"><span>Neck and shoulder pain. </span></li>
 	<li aria-level="2"><b>The Fix</b><span>: Use the 20-20-20 rule. Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.</span></li>
</ul>
<span>Learn more about </span><a href="/blog/causes-of-eye-strain-symptoms"><span>causes &amp; symptoms of eye strain</span></a><span>.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h4><b>Dry Eye Syndrome</b></h4>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Tears are not just water. They are a complex mix of oil and water. They create a smooth surface for light to enter the eye. If the eyes are dry, the surface becomes bumpy. This scatters light and causes blur. </span>

<b>The Blink Test</b><span>: Does your vision clear up immediately after a hard blink? If yes, the cause is likely dry eyes, not your glasses prescription. Artificial tear drops can help fix this.</span>

<span>Losing the sharpness of your sight is unsettling. You might rub your eyes. You might blink a few times. But the haze remains. To be fair, blurry vision is the most common eye symptom people report to doctors.</span>
<h2><b>Why Is My Vision Blurry All Of A Sudden? The Red Flags</b></h2>
<span>Most eye problems happen slowly. But some happen fast. Doctors use a simple rule: </span><b>Sudden vs Gradual.</b><span> If your vision changes instantly, it is a red flag. </span>

<span>You must check for these emergency signs immediately.</span>
<h3><b>Go to A&amp;E or an eye hospital if you notice:</b></h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Sudden Onset:</b><span> The blurriness happens instantly. It feels like a switch flipped.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>One Eye Only:</b><span> If only one eye is blurry, this is serious. It can be a sign of a stroke or a retinal detachment.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>The Curtain:</b><span> You see a dark shadow or curtain falling over part of your vision. This suggests the retina has pulled away from the back of the eye.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Pain and Halos:</b><span> You feel severe eye pain. You see coloured rings (halos) around lights. You feel sick or vomit. This is a sign of Closed-Angle Glaucoma.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Stroke Signs:</b><span> Your face droops. Your speech is slurred. You feel weak on one side of your body.</span></li>
</ul>
<b>If you have these symptoms, do not wait. Seek medical help straight away.</b>
<h2><b>How Do Doctors Diagnose Blurry Vision?</b></h2>
<span>Do not guess with your sight. An eye exam is painless and thorough. Here is what the doctor will do to find the cause.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Visual Acuity Test:</b><span> You read letters on a chart. This measures how sharp your vision is.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Slit Lamp Exam:</b><span> The doctor uses a special microscope. They look at the front of your eye. They check the cornea, iris, and lens.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Dilation:</b><span> They might put drops in your eyes. This widens the pupil. It allows the doctor to see the retina at the back.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Tonometry:</b><span> This checks the pressure inside your eye. It might be a gentle touch or a puff of air. This test is for Glaucoma.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><b>How Is Blurry Vision Treated?</b></h2>
<span>Once the doctor knows the cause, treatment is straightforward. Here are the common ways to fix blurry vision.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Glasses and Contact Lenses </b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>This is the most common fix. Corrective lenses adjust how light enters the eye. They fix myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism. For people over 40, reading glasses or varifocals help with presbyopia.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Lubricating Drops</b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>For dry eyes, artificial tears are brilliant. They add moisture to the eye. They smooth out the visual surface. For digital strain, using these drops during the workday helps reduce fatigue.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Medical Management </b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>If diabetes or high blood pressure is the cause, treating the body helps the eyes. Controlling blood sugar can reduce lens swelling. Managing blood pressure protects the retinal vessels.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Surgery </b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Some conditions need a procedure.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="2"><b>Cataract Surgery:</b><span> The cloudy lens is removed. It is replaced with a clear artificial lens. This restores clear sight.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="2"><b>LASIK:</b><span> A laser reshapes the cornea. This removes the need for glasses.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><b>Conclusion</b></h2>
<span>Blurry vision is your body telling you something. Usually, it just means you need a rest or a new prescription. But you should never ignore it. Vision is precious. Small changes can signal big health issues. </span>

<span>If your vision is fuzzy, do not struggle in silence. Book a comprehensive eye exam. </span>

<span>Let an expert check your eye health. Getting the right help can make the world look bright and clear again.</span>
