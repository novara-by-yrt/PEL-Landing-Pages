<p>If your skin has started looking a bit tired lately, less plump than it used to be, or just generally dull no matter how much moisturiser you use, there's a decent chance you've come across Profhilo in your research. It gets mentioned constantly alongside fillers and skin boosters, which can make it hard to understand what it actually does.</p>
<p>So here is the straightforward version: what Profhilo is, how it works, and whether it is actually the right fit for what you are trying to fix.</p>
<h2>What Is Profhilo?</h2>
<p><a href="/non-surgical-injectables-medical-aesthetic/profhilo">Profhilo</a> is an injectable treatment built around a very high concentration of pure hyaluronic acid, but its job is not to add volume or reshape anything. Unlike a filler, which is placed precisely to plump a specific area like the lips or cheeks, Profhilo is injected at just a handful of points and then spreads through the surrounding tissue on its own.</p>
<p>That spreading action is the whole point. Rather than sculpting a feature, it works to improve the general condition of the skin sitting over the top, hydration, firmness, and elasticity. This is why it is usually described as a bio-remodelling treatment rather than a filler or a typical skin booster. It is doing something a bit different from both.</p>
<p>At Perfect Skin Studio, we use the BAP (Bio Aesthetic Points) injection technique, which places Profhilo at five specific points on each side of the face for the most even distribution and natural-looking results.</p>
<h2>How Does Profhilo Work?</h2>
<p>Profhilo uses hyaluronic acid without the chemical cross-linking that gives dermal fillers their thicker, more structural consistency. That lack of cross-linking is exactly why it can move through tissue rather than staying put where it is injected.</p>
<p>From the injection points, the product disperses across a much wider area, working on the skin's hydration levels and, over time, stimulating the skin's own collagen and elastin production. That is a meaningfully different mechanism from a filler, which stays localised and structural. Profhilo is aiming at skin quality broadly, not a specific contour, which is also why the results tend to look more like your skin, but better, rather than an obvious cosmetic change.</p>

<h2>Is Profhilo a Skin Booster?</h2>
<p>Sort of, but with an important caveat. It gets grouped under the skin booster umbrella because, like other skin boosters, it improves skin quality rather than adding shape or volume. Where it differs is in concentration and mechanism. Most skin boosters use a lower concentration of hyaluronic acid and stay more localised, while Profhilo's high concentration and lack of cross-linking let it spread and remodel tissue more broadly. In clinical terms it is more accurately described as a bio-remodelling injectable, with skin booster working as a simpler, more familiar label for the same general category.</p>

<h2>Which Is Better, a Skin Booster or Profhilo?</h2>
<p>Neither is objectively better. It comes down to what your skin actually needs. A standard skin booster tends to suit someone who wants a light hydration refresh and does not have significant laxity or crepiness to deal with. Profhilo tends to be the better fit for more noticeable skin laxity, dullness, or early textural changes, particularly from your mid-thirties onward, since its remodelling effect goes a step further than surface hydration alone.</p>
<p>If you are not sure which category your skin falls into, a consultation with our team at Perfect Skin Studio is the most useful next step. Comparing the two in the abstract is far less useful than having one of our doctors assess your skin directly.</p>
<h2>What Areas Can Profhilo Treat?</h2>
<p>Profhilo is most associated with the face, but it is regularly used elsewhere too. At Perfect Skin Studio we treat:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Face:</strong> general hydration, texture, and overall skin quality improvements, including cheeks, nasolabial folds, laugh lines, chin, perioral lines, and jawline.</li>
<li><strong>Neck:</strong> a common area for early dryness and elasticity loss that often gets overlooked.</li>
<li><strong>Decolletage:</strong> the upper chest picks up sun damage and dryness over time in much the same way the face does.</li>
<li><strong>Hands:</strong> skin here thins with age, making veins and tendons more prominent, an area Profhilo can help soften.</li>
</ul>
<p>Which areas make sense for you depends on where your skin concerns are showing up, which our doctors will discuss with you at your consultation.</p>
<h2>What Happens During Treatment at Perfect Skin Studio?</h2>
<p>Before anything happens, one of our experienced doctors will assess your skin and talk through your goals, medical history, and expectations. We use the BAP (Bio Aesthetic Points) technique, injecting Profhilo at five points per side of the face. The session itself typically takes 30 to 45 minutes.</p>
<p>You will likely notice small raised bumps right where the injections went in. These are expected and settle within about 24 hours as the product spreads through the tissue.</p>
<h3>Does It Hurt?</h3>
<p>Pain tolerance varies from person to person, but since it involves several injection points, some mild discomfort during the session is normal. We offer topical anaesthetic cream beforehand, and our doctors are experienced in making the process as comfortable as possible.</p>
<h3>What Is the Downtime?</h3>
<p>One of the appeals of Profhilo is that it does not come with the extended recovery that surgical procedures involve. In the short term you might notice small bumps at the injection sites, some redness, mild swelling, or light bruising. These typically resolve within 24 hours, though individual healing time varies.</p>
<p>We advise avoiding intense exercise and alcohol for 24 hours after treatment, and avoiding touching or rubbing the treated area for the first 6 hours. Makeup should be left off for at least 12 hours.</p>
<h2>When Will You See Results, and How Long Do They Last?</h2>
<p>Do not expect an immediate transformation on the day of treatment. Profhilo works progressively as the hyaluronic acid disperses and stimulates the skin over the following weeks. The standard protocol at Perfect Skin Studio involves two initial sessions spaced about a month apart, which gives the treatment its full effect, followed by maintenance sessions roughly every six to twelve months.</p>
<p>Results vary by individual, influenced by your age, skin condition, sun exposure, and lifestyle. It is more realistic to think of Profhilo as something you maintain on a schedule, similar to how you might think about ongoing skincare, rather than a single fix.</p>
<h2>What Age Is Best for Profhilo?</h2>
<p>There is no strict cutoff, but Profhilo is most commonly recommended for people aged 35 and older, once mild skin laxity, dullness, or early crepiness starts becoming noticeable. That said, age alone is not the deciding factor. Someone in their late twenties with early sun damage or skin dehydration might reasonably benefit, while someone older with more significant volume loss might get more from a filler, or a combination of both. This is very much an individual assessment rather than a fixed age bracket, and our doctors at Perfect Skin Studio will advise accordingly.</p>
<h2>Who Should Avoid Profhilo?</h2>
<p>Profhilo is not automatically right for everyone. Certain medical conditions, allergies, pregnancy, breastfeeding, active skin infections, and some medications can affect whether injectable treatments are appropriate. Being upfront with your doctor about your full medical history, current medications, and any previous aesthetic treatments is important. Minimally invasive does not mean risk-free for everyone, and our team will always assess your suitability before proceeding.</p>
<h2>What Are the Potential Risks?</h2>
<p>While Profhilo is generally considered safe, it is worth going in with a realistic picture. Possible side effects include:</p>
<ul><li>Redness and mild swelling at the injection sites</li>
<li>Small temporary bumps (Profhilo lumps) that usually settle within 24 hours</li>
<li>Light bruising</li>
<li>Itching or mild discomfort</li>
<li>Infection, in rare cases</li>
<li>Allergic reaction, in rare cases</li>
</ul>
<p>Serious complications are uncommon. At Perfect Skin Studio, all treatments are carried out by medically qualified doctors with extensive experience in injectable treatments.</p>
<h2>What Are the Disadvantages of Profhilo?</h2>
<p>It is worth going in with a realistic picture rather than just the benefits.</p>
<ul><li><strong>It is not a quick fix.</strong> Results build gradually over weeks, so it does not suit anyone wanting an instant change before an event.</li>
<li><strong>It does not add volume.</strong> If your concern is lost fullness in the cheeks or under-eye hollowing, Profhilo will not address that. A filler would be the more appropriate treatment.</li>
<li><strong>Results are subtle by design.</strong> For some people that is the appeal. For others expecting a more visible transformation, it can feel underwhelming.</li>
<li><strong>It requires maintenance.</strong> Ongoing sessions every six to twelve months are typically needed to sustain results.</li>
<li><strong>Results vary between individuals.</strong> Skin condition, age, and lifestyle all affect how well it works and how long it lasts.</li>
</ul>
<p>None of this makes it a bad treatment. It just means it is suited to a specific goal, improving overall skin quality, rather than being an all-purpose solution.</p>
