<span>Many people are tired of dealing with under eye wrinkles and are looking for effective ways to regain a more youthful appearance. </span>

<span>Under eye wrinkles are some of the most noticeable signs of ageing. As we grow older, our skin loses its elasticity and needs special care. </span><b>The skin around the eyes is thin, which makes it more prone to developing wrinkles. </b><span>The fragile skin under our eyes makes it easier for lines and wrinkles to become visible. Fortunately, there are ways to slow down the formation of these wrinkles and, in some cases, even prevent them.</span>

<span>Getting a young, refreshed appearance doesn't have to take a lot of time.</span><b> If your under eye lines don't go away even after using different eye creams, it might be time to look into non-surgical procedures that have more promising results.</b><span> Here, we provide a thorough guide on the primary causes of under eye wrinkles and suggest the best treatments to deal with them. Keep reading to learn </span><b>how to get rid of under eye wrinkles</b><span>!</span>
<h2>What Causes Wrinkles Around Eyes?</h2>
<span>Crow's feet, or wrinkles around the eyes, can appear as an expected consequence of ageing. However, other factors can also be involved like decreased skin elasticity, smoking, sun exposure, etc. Below are the main causes of under eye wrinkles:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Sun exposure and UV rays</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Natural ageing process</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Decreased collagen production</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Smoking</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Environmental factors</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Genetic traits</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Repetitive movements and expressions</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Poor diet</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Lack of sleep</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Dehydration</span></li>
</ul>
<h2>How To Get Rid of Under Eye Wrinkles? - Cosmetic Procedures</h2>
<span>Now that we know the causes that contribute to under eye lines, let's explore effective treatments. While certain factors, such as ageing and facial expressions, are unavoidable, we can still minimise the appearance of these lines. There are various cosmetic procedures available to help reduce the appearance of under eye wrinkles. It's important to carefully consider the options and consult a qualified professional to determine the best approach for your individual needs. Below, we have outlined some of the most frequently used options.</span>
<h3>Dermal Fillers</h3>
<span>Dermal or soft tissue fillers, commonly known as </span><a href="/treatments/non-surgical-injectables-medical-aesthetic/tear-trough-treatment-london"><span>under eye fillers</span></a><span> and tear trough fillers, are used to restore fullness and volume to the face. They can enhance the under-eye area by injecting hyaluronic acid or collagen to plump the skin and smooth out wrinkles.</span>

<span>For all tear trough dermal filler operations, Teosyal Redensity2 is used, a brand specifically created for smooth, natural outcomes. Seek guidance from your doctor to select the most suitable option.</span>

<span>Fillers perform by filling in wrinkles and hollow areas on the face, resulting in smoother, more youthful-looking skin. The duration of results varies depending on the type of filler and treatment area, lasting from 6 months to a few years and usually performed under topical anaesthesia.</span>
<h3>Lasers</h3>
<span>Laser treatments use light to create micro-injuries in the skin to promote collagen and elastin production for firmer, smoother skin with reduced under eye wrinkles.</span>

<span>For the treatment of under-eye wrinkles, a variety of laser treatments are available, such as non-ablative lasers that heat the lower layers of skin to increase collagen formation and ablative lasers that remove the top layer of skin. To choose the best method, weigh the benefits and downsides of each type with a doctor. Dr Sabrina Shah Desai, a leading oculoplastic surgeon, uses the “</span><a href="/non-surgical-cosmetic/ultraclear-laser"><span>Ultraclear Laser</span></a><span>.” It is a cold ablative fibre-optic Erbium laser that rejuvenates your skin health by resurfacing skin.</span>

<span>Laser therapy for wrinkles around eyes typically requires 3-6 treatments every 3-4 weeks, with the final results becoming visible after 4-5 days after just 1 treatment. Many individuals notice significant improvements in skin tone and texture, as well as a reduction in fine lines and wrinkles.</span>
<h3>Microneedling</h3>
<span>Microneedling is a minimally invasive treatment that uses small needles to create slight skin injuries. This method helps to lessen fine lines and wrinkles around the eyes by encouraging the formation of new skin cells and collagen.</span>

<span>Microneedling has many benefits like it helps to improve skin texture, tone, and suppleness along with less creases and lines. Although, there are also certain risks connected to this treatment, like skin discolouration, infection chances, or scarring. </span>

<span>It is crucial to have a thorough discussion about these risks with your doctor prior to undergoing microneedling.</span>

<span>The procedure typically takes less than an hour, and a course of 1-5 treatments is recommended, 3-6 weeks apart. This option offers less downtime compared to surgical alternatives and provides long-lasting results, as the skin-tightening effects can last up to 2-5 years.</span>
<h3></h3>
<h3>Wrinkle Relaxing Injections</h3>
<span>Wrinkle relaxing injections are a non-surgical technique that permanently reduces wrinkles and lines. During the process, your practitioner will inject a small dose of a neurotoxin into your skin. This relaxes the muscles, reduces dynamic and static wrinkles, and gives your eyes a brighter, younger appearance. </span>

<span>These treatments are helpful at smoothing out wrinkles and significantly improving the overall appearance of your eyes, making them appear more youthful and revitalised.</span>
<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<span>Under eye wrinkles are a common sign of ageing and can be caused by various factors such as sun exposure, smoking, and repetitive movements. Simple lifestyle changes like eating well and getting enough sleep can help prevent wrinkles, but there are also cosmetic operations that can successfully lessen the look of wrinkles.</span>

<span>A more youthful appearance can be attained by using procedures like fillers, lasers, and microneedling, which improve skin texture and minimise fine wrinkles. For best results, it's crucial to speak with an experienced doctor to choose the best treatment and to ensure that you follow the recommended aftercare. </span>
