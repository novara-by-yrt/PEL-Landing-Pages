They stimulate fibroblasts, induce cell renewal, increase elasticity, and promote collagen production as well as hydration of the skin.</span>

<span>For individuals who have experienced swelling or adverse reactions from conventional fillers, this treatment serves as a safe alternative. Additionally, those concerned about potential side effects can benefit from its regenerative properties. <h2>Top Benefits of Polynucleotide Injections</h2>
<span>Polynucleotide treatments offer several benefits for improving the skin's health and appearance.</span>
<h3>1. Skin Rejuvenation</h3>
<span>Polynucleotide injectables help skin healing and regeneration by stimulating fibroblasts. This is again associated with the production of collagen and elastin that results in enlarged skin elasticity and tone the firmness and hydration of the skin should be improved. It helps in reducing </span>fine lines and wrinkles<span>, making the skin look younger and refreshed.</span>
<h3>2. Scar Treatment</h3>
<span>Polynucleotides have been known to treat traumatic scars, acne scars, and surgical scars. They give good results in tissue regeneration, improving the texture of the skin and reducing scars' appearance.</span>
<h3>3. Reduce Hyperpigmentation</h3>
<span>Polynucleotides can also be used to treat hyperpigmented lesions. Hyperpigmentation generally occurs when there is melanin overproduction. This condition can be countered by using polynucleotides. Polynucleotide treatment might also increase skin cell turnover, repair damaged cells, and create a more uniform tone of the skin.</span>
<h3>4. Improved Skin Texture</h3>
<span>Polynucleotides can improve skin elasticity, firmness, and tone. They also help reduce the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, resulting in smoother and more youthful skin.</span>
<h3>5. Increased Hydration</h3>
<span>Polynucleotides enhance the skin’s ability to hold water, which gives a plumper and much smoother appearance. All of this hydration even brightens and beautifies the skin.</span>
<h3>6. Decreased inflammation</h3>
<span>These molecules calm the skin, being efficacious in reducing redness, irritation, and inflammation, thus resulting in more harmonised and calmer skin.</span>
<h3>7. Antioxidant Protection</h3>
<span>Polynucleotides offer protection against oxidative injury from external forces such as ultraviolet radiation and pollution. Through this means, premature skin ageing is prevented, and skin health is preserved.</span>
<h3>8. Tissue Repair and Regeneration</h3>
<span>Polynucleotides stimulate the activity of fibroblasts that produce collagen meant for tissue repair after damage and maintaining the dermis structure.</span>
<h3>9. Reduced Appearance of Dark Circles </h3>
<span>Polynucleotides effectively alleviate the appearance of dark circles beneath the eyes, imparting a rejuvenated and refreshed feeling.</span>
<h3>10. Plumping Effect</h3>
<span>Polynucleotide treatment plumps the skin of under-eye areas, rendering them with a more youthful, vibrant appearance.</span>
<h3>11. Enhanced Results of Other Treatments</h3>
<span>Polynucleotides can amplify the outcomes of other aesthetic procedures, such as laser treatments, by enhancing skin repair and regeneration.</span>
<h3>12. Minimal Downtime</h3>
<span>Compared to other injectables, polynucleotide treatments have little downtime, making them a convenient option for many patients.</span>
<h3>13. Smoothing Fine Lines and Wrinkles</h3>
<span>While ageing is a natural process, polynucleotide injections can help soften its effects. They boost the collagen levels in the epidermis and smooth fine lines and wrinkles, producing a more youthful and rejuvenated appearance through soft, fuller-looking skin.</span>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<strong>01. What to expect after Polynucleotide treatment?</strong>

<span>After polynucleotide treatment, a gel containing these molecules is injected into the skin using a tiny needle or a soft, blunt tube called a cannula. Right after the treatment, you might see temporary puffiness, redness, or small bumps in the treated area. These effects usually go away within 1 to 3 days, depending on the treatment method and area.</span>

<strong>02. What do Polynucleotides do?</strong>

<span>Polynucleotides stimulate fibroblast cells which are responsible for producing connective tissues such as collagen and elastin, proteins that keep skin firm, plump, and young. They also support the hydration and better alive cells with their action on the extracellular matrix that will improve the skin.</span>

<strong>03. Are there any risks with Polynucleotides?</strong>

<span>Although polynucleotides are safe and compatible with the human body, allergic reactions can happen in rare cases. Symptoms might include rashes, swelling, or, in severe cases, an allergic response like anaphylaxis.</span>

<strong>04. Who should avoid Polynucleotide treatments?</strong>

<span>Polynucleotide treatments aren’t suitable for people with known allergies to any ingredients in the formulation. It’s always a good idea to consult with a doctor and discuss your medical history before starting this treatment.</span>

<strong>05. Can I sleep on my side after Polynucleotide treatment?</strong>

<span>After your treatment, you should:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Avoid touching the treated area for at least 6 hours.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Try not to lie down for 6 hours after the procedure.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Sleep on your back that night to avoid putting pressure on the treated area.</span></li>
