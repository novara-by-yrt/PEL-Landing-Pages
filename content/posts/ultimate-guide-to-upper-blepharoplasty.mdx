<span>The skin ageing process causes everyone to experience volume loss, diminished elasticity, and suppleness. It can lead to hood formation on the upper eyelids. Eye puffiness is also the result of extra fat and muscle weakness around the eyes.</span>

<span>Upper eyelid blepharoplasty, or Eyelid lift surgery, can greatly improve the look of your eyes and make them more youthful. It mainly targets issues such as excess fat, skin, and muscle tissues on the upper eyelids through surgery.</span>

<span>In this detailed article, we'll provide all the information you require, from understanding upper eyelid surgery to its possible risks, benefits, and costs. </span>
<h2>Understanding the Upper Eyelid Blepharoplasty Procedure</h2>
<a href="/surgical/cosmetic-eyelid-surgery/upper-eyelid-lift-surgery-blepharoplasty"><span>Upper Eyelid Blepharoplasty</span></a><span> is frequently performed using local or twilight anaesthesia in a day case. The process starts with a consultation with your surgeon (ideally choose and trained and experienced oculoplastic surgeon). During this discussion, you will express your issues and clarify your expected results.</span>

<span>When it's time for your surgery, the doctor will examine the skin and tissues around your eyes and make some markings on your skin as a guide. Then, a shield may be placed over your eye to protect it during the operation. The surgeon will make strategic cuts to adjust or remove any extra skin, fat, or muscle tissue. </span>

<span>Blepharoplasty is a delicate procedure that needs a skilled and experienced surgeon to achieve your expected results. Dr Sabrina Shah-Desai, an oculoplastic surgeon, uses her signature "Zip Stitch" technique to minimise scarring during Upper Eyelid Surgery. The incision is carefully placed in the natural eyelid crease, resulting in an invisible scar. Patients can expect long-lasting results, with the effects of the surgery lasting up to 10 years.</span>

Through upper blepharoplasty, individuals can bid farewell to tired eyes, regain visual clarity, and rejuvenate their overall facial appearance. </span>

 	<li><b>Extra Fat: </b><span>Fat accumulation in the upper eyelid can create puffiness and a tired look. The procedure can address this extra fat, resulting in a smoother, more defined eyelid crease.</span></li>
 	<li><b>Improved Vision: </b><span>Sometimes, when too much skin on the upper eyelids makes it hard to see, upper eyelid surgery can improve the range of vision and how clearly you can see.</span></li>

<h2>Upper Blepharoplasty Risks </h2>
<span>Blepharoplasty has some risks and potential side effects, like any surgical surgery, including the possibility of infection, slow healing, and dry eyes. The potential risks associated with Upper Eyelid Surgery include:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Swelling can be severe in the lower eyelids for 2-3 weeks, even if only the upper lids are operated on.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Bruising, which can last from 1-3 weeks.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Blurring of vision for the first week after the surgery.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Feelings of irritation, dryness, or excessive tearing may persist for 6 weeks or more.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Temporary or permanent reduction in blink.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Visibility of the upper eyelid scar can take 3-6 months to fade. Although 99% of scars hidden in the crease are barely visible, 1% may be pale, thickened, or depressed and may have a different colour.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Swelling and tightness in the eyelids after surgery may cause minor discomfort or pain.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Temporary dry eye, which can occasionally worsen pre-existing dry eye or blepharitis and may present as eyelid lumps or chalazion that may require further treatment.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2>Upper Blepharoplasty Recovery and Aftercare</h2>
<span>To get the greatest results following upper blepharoplasty, a well-thought-out and well-conducted recovery time is necessary. Here, we will address the </span><a href="/blog/blepharoplasty-eyelid-recovery-guide"><span>upper blepharoplasty recovery process,</span></a><span> such as post-operative care that needs to be followed immediately, when to resume everyday activities, scar care, follow-up consultations, and long-term factors.</span>
<h3>Post-Surgery Recovery and Care:</h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>It's normal to feel some discomfort after surgery. Your doctor may give you pain medication to help.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Swelling and bruising around your eyes are common after surgery. </span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Using cold compresses can help reduce swelling, and your doctor will prescribe pain medication if needed. You will also get lubricant eye drops and an antibiotic ointment to apply.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Your eye area may feel sensitive, tight, or painful.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Clean the area around your eyes as instructed by your surgeon, but avoid massaging your eyes. Eye drops may be recommended to maintain moisture in your eyes.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>Recovery Timeline:</h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Most people can go back to work and do non-strenuous activities within 5-10 days.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>It's important to avoid intense exercise and heavy lifting for a few weeks to prevent complications.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Taking care of your surgical cuts is crucial for proper recovery and to minimise scarring. Your doctor will give you specific instructions for this.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Swelling could last up to six weeks or more in some cases. Swelling takes time to gradually go down, but you can expedite the process by connecting with Dr Shah-Desai's team to do weekly facial lymphatic drainage treatments.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>Follow-Up Appointments:</h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>It's important to go to all your follow-up appointments with your doctor. They will check how you're doing, remove any stitches, and answer any questions you have.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3>Long-Term Care:</h3>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Scarring is normal after surgery, but your doctor will make incisions in natural folds to minimise visible scarring.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>It's important to take care of your skin and protect it from the sun to keep your eyelids looking good.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Leading a healthy lifestyle, including a good diet and not smoking, can help maintain one's results in the long run.</span></li>
</ul>
