<h2><b>What Are Hooded Eyes?</b></h2>
<span>Hooded eyes are an eye shape where a fold of skin sits over the eyelid crease, covering part of the eyelid and sometimes reducing the visible lid space.</span>

<span>This fold creates a hooded appearance that can make the eyes look deeper set or slightly shaded.</span>

<span>People can have hooded eyes for different reasons:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>Genetics</b><span> - Many individuals are born with this eye shape.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Ageing</b><span> - Skin gradually loses elasticity, causing the upper eyelid to fold downward.</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Brow structure</b><span> - A low brow position or a prominent brow bone can create a hooded look.</span></li>
</ul>
<span>For many individuals, hooded eyes are simply a natural facial trait rather than a medical issue.</span>

<h2><b>Why Are Hooded Eyes Attractive?</b></h2>
<span>Several features make hooded eyes appealing to many people.</span>
<h3><b>1. They Create a Mysterious Look</b></h3>
<span>One reason why hooded eyes are attractive is the depth they add to the eyes.</span>

<span>Because the eyelid is partially covered, the eyes often appear softer and more expressive. This can create a bedroom eyes” effect that many people associate with allure.</span>
<h3><b>2. They Add Character to the Face</b></h3>
<span>Faces with distinctive features are often considered more memorable.</span>

<span>Hooded eyes can add individuality and personality, especially when balanced with other facial features such as strong brows or high cheekbones.</span>
<h3><b>3. They Are Common Among Celebrities</b></h3>
<span>Many actors, models, and public figures have hooded eyes. Their popularity has helped normalise and celebrate this eye shape.</span>

<span>Seeing these features in media often changes public perception and reinforces the idea that hooded eyes are attractive.</span>
<h3><b>4. They Create a Natural Shadow</b></h3>
<span>The slight fold above the eyelid creates subtle shading. This can add more dimension to the eyes without makeup.</span>

<span>In photography and film, this natural depth can enhance facial expression.</span>
<h2><b>Are Hooded Eyes Considered Attractive Across Cultures?</b></h2>
<span>Beauty standards vary widely across cultures.</span>

<span>Modern beauty trends increasingly emphasise diversity in facial features, meaning more people appreciate different eye shapes.</span>

<span>Research and aesthetic discussions suggest that facial attractiveness is influenced by overall harmony rather than a single feature.</span>
