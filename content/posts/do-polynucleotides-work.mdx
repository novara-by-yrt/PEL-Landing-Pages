<span>If you’ve been keeping an eye on new skin treatments, you’ve probably heard the word polynucleotides. They’re rapidly becoming popular in regenerative aesthetic treatments. These powerful molecules were first utilised in medical treatments, such as wound healing, but are now being applied to enhance skin health &amp; appearance.</span>

<span>They don’t just sit on the surface. These molecules actually work deep within the skin, encouraging your cells to repair, hydrate and rebuild themselves over time. But with so many skincare trends coming and going, it’s natural to ask, do polynucleotides really work, or is it just another hype? Let’s break it down.</span>
<h2><strong>What Are Polynucleotides?</strong></h2>
<span>Polynucleotides are natural molecules composed of nucleotide chains. They’re most commonly sourced from salmon DNA due to its strong compatibility with human skin and tissue. These molecules are purified and processed. They are generally used in injectable treatments to stimulate the natural regeneration processes of your skin.</span>

<span>They are biocompatible, biodegradable, and designed to repair &amp; regenerate tissue at a cellular level.</span>
<h2><strong>Do Polynucleotides Work?</strong></h2>
<span>Yes, polynucleotides are known to be effective in improving skin quality, but the results take a bit of time to show. They work by supporting your skin’s natural repair process, helping it produce more collagen &amp; elastin while boosting hydration. Some people start to see changes after a few weeks, but the full results usually appear over a few months with consistent treatment.</span>
<h3><strong>Polynucleotides Results</strong></h3>
<h2><strong>How Do Polynucleotides Work?</strong></h2>
<span>Polynucleotides work deep within the skin to repair, restore, and improve the overall quality of your skin. They don’t add volume like fillers. Instead, they act as biostimulators, which encourage your skin to heal and rebuild itself from the inside.</span>

<span>When injected just under the surface using a fine needle, polynucleotides trigger fibroblasts (the skin cells responsible for producing collagen and elastin). It helps tighten the skin, reduce fine lines, and gradually boost elasticity.</span>

<span>They also boost hydration by attracting water molecules, improving skin texture, calming inflammation &amp; supporting tissue repair in damaged or ageing areas.</span>

<span>A major benefit is their ability to neutralise free radicals, which helps slow down visible signs of age.</span>

<span>You can apply this treatment to specific areas, like under the eyes. This works beautifully on delicate areas that require a gentle touch while still delivering noticeable rejuvenation.</span>
<h2><strong>Are Polynucleotides Safe?</strong></h2>
<span>Yes, polynucleotide treatments are generally very safe. In skin treatments, polynucleotides are usually taken from natural sources like fish, but they go through a careful purification process to make them safe and suitable for the human body.</span>

<span>Polynucleotide treatment is generally considered safe when performed by a trained and skilled medical practitioner. Like with any injectable treatment, there can be some mild side effects, such as bruising or swelling.</span>

<span>Since polynucleotides are made from substances already found in our body, most people tolerate them well and allergic reactions are very rare. Still, it’s important to have your treatment done at a trusted clinic with the right expertise, so any risks can be handled safely and quickly if needed.</span>

<span>Polynucleotide treatments work gradually, as they help your skin heal and rebuild itself from the inside. You may see early signs of improvement within a couple of weeks, but full results take a bit more time as collagen and elastin levels increase.</span>

<ul><li aria-level="1"><b>1 to 2 weeks: </b><span>You might notice your skin feels more hydrated and looks slightly brighter. These are the first signs that the treatment is starting to work.</span></li>

<span>Everyone has a different skin type, so the timing of results can vary based on age, skin condition, and overall health.</span>
