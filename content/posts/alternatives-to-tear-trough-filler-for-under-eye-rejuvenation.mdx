<span>If you are looking for alternatives to tear trough filler for under-eye rejuvenation, the best options include treatments like polynucleotides, PRP/PRF, laser therapy, RF microneedling, and skin boosters such as Sunekos and Jalupro. </span>

<span>These treatments focus on improving skin quality, hydration, and collagen production rather than simply adding volume. They are ideal for concerns like fine lines, dark circles, thin skin, and mild hollowness. Choosing the right option depends on the cause of your under-eye concern, making a personalised approach essential for natural and long-lasting results.</span>
<h2><b>What Are Under-Eye Injectables?</b></h2>
<span>Under-eye injectables are treatments designed to improve the appearance of the delicate skin beneath the eyes. These include both volumising fillers and non-filler options such as skin boosters and regenerative injectables.</span>

<span>Unlike tear trough filler, which adds volume to fill hollows, non-filler under-eye injectables focus on improving the quality of the skin itself. They work by:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Increasing hydration within the skin</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Stimulating collagen and elastin production</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Strengthening thin or fragile skin</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Reducing fine lines and improving texture</span></li>
</ul>
<span>These treatments are especially useful for patients whose concerns are related to skin quality rather than volume loss.</span>

<span>Understanding this difference is key when choosing the right under-eye treatment.</span>
<h2><b>Under-Eye Injectables Explained: Polynucleotides, Sunekos, Jalupro and NCTF®</b></h2>
<span>The under-eye area is one of the most delicate and complex parts of the face. It is often the first area where signs of fatigue, ageing, and stress become visible. Many patients assume that tear trough filler is the best or only solution for dark circles, hollowness, or tired-looking eyes.</span>

<span>However, this is not always the case.</span>

<span>In many patients, the main issue is not volume loss but poor skin quality. Thin, dehydrated, or damaged skin can create shadows, fine lines, and a hollow appearance even when volume is not significantly reduced.</span>

<span>This is where modern under-eye injectables offer a different approach.</span>

<span>Treatments such as polynucleotides, Sunekos, Jalupro, and NCTF® are designed to improve the skin itself rather than simply filling space. These treatments are often referred to as </span><b>skin boosters or regenerative injectables</b><span>, and they focus on restoring skin health over time.</span>

<span>Instead of creating immediate volume, they work gradually to strengthen the skin, improve hydration, and support natural collagen production—leading to more natural and longer-lasting results.</span>
<h2><b>Why Under-Eye Skin Needs a Different Approach</b></h2>
<span>The skin under the eyes behaves very differently from the rest of the face, which is why treatment must be carefully selected.</span>

<span>This area is:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Extremely thin, making veins and shadows more visible</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Prone to dehydration, which worsens fine lines and dullness</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>More sensitive to inflammation, leading to puffiness or irritation</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Slower to regenerate collagen, especially with age</span></li>
</ul>
<span>Because of these factors, simply adding volume with filler can sometimes lead to unnatural results, swelling, or persistent puffiness, especially in patients with thin or delicate skin.</span>

<span>A more effective approach often focuses on:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Improving skin thickness and resilience</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Supporting collagen and elastin production</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Enhancing hydration from within</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Reducing inflammation and improving skin quality</span></li>
</ul>
<span>This is why non-filler under-eye treatments are becoming increasingly popular.</span>
<h2><b>Types of Under-Eye Injectables: Non-Filler Alternatives to Tear Trough Filler</b></h2>
 

<span>Non-filler under-eye injectables focus on improving skin quality rather than adding volume. These treatments work by boosting hydration, stimulating collagen, and strengthening the delicate under-eye area for more natural results over time.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Polynucleotides – Deep Regeneration and Skin Repair</b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Polynucleotides are considered one of the most advanced regenerative treatments for under-eye rejuvenation.</span>

<span>They work at a cellular level by repairing damaged skin tissue and improving the overall health of the skin. Unlike fillers, they do not add volume but instead improve the structure and function of the skin itself.</span>

<span>They help to:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Stimulate collagen and elastin production</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Improve skin thickness and strength</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Reduce inflammation and redness</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Support tissue healing and regeneration</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Best suited for:</b>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Thin, crepey or fragile under-eye skin</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Patients with previous filler complications</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Puffiness caused by poor skin quality</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Individuals seeking a natural, regenerative approach</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Result:</b><span>A gradual improvement in skin quality, with smoother, stronger, and healthier-looking under-eye skin over time.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Sunekos – Collagen and Elastin Reconstruction</b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Sunekos is a unique injectable that combines hyaluronic acid with a patented blend of amino acids.</span>

<span>What makes Sunekos different is its ability to stimulate both collagen and elastin production, which are essential for maintaining skin firmness and elasticity.</span>

<span>This treatment works particularly well when early signs of ageing begin to appear.</span>

<span>It helps to:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Improve skin elasticity and firmness</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Smooth fine lines and “crinkling” under the eyes</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Strengthen thinning skin</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Restore a more youthful skin structure</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Best suited for:</b>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Fine lines under the eyes</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Early skin laxity</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Mild hollowing caused by thinning skin</span> </li>
</ul>
<b>Result:</b><span> Firmer, smoother, and more elastic skin with visible improvement in texture and quality.</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1">
<h3><b>Jalupro – Gentle Collagen Support and Hydration</b></h3>
</li>
</ul>
<span>Jalupro is a milder injectable treatment that focuses on improving hydration and supporting collagen production.</span>

<span>It contains amino acids that act as building blocks for collagen, along with hyaluronic acid to boost moisture levels in the skin.</span>

<span>Compared to other treatments, Jalupro offers a softer and more subtle approach, making it ideal for early intervention or maintenance.</span>

<span>It helps to:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Improve hydration in the under-eye area</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Smooth very fine lines</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Enhance overall skin texture</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Support long-term skin health</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Best suited for:</b>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Younger patients</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Early or mild skin changes</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Maintenance after stronger treatments</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Result:</b><span>A refreshed, hydrated appearance with subtle but noticeable improvements in skin quality.</span>

 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Enhancing skin vitality</span> </li>

<b>Best suited for:</b>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Dull or tired-looking under-eyes</span></li>

<span>A personalised consultation is essential to assess skin quality, anatomy, and treatment suitability.</span>
