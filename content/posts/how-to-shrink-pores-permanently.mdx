<h2><b>What Are Pores?</b></h2>
<span>Pores are small openings on your skin that allow sweat and oil to escape. Each pore is connected to a sebaceous gland that produces sebum (oil). Although pores are an essential part of healthy skin function, they can look larger when clogged with oil, dirt or dead skin cells.</span>

<span>Some people naturally have larger pores, especially those with oily or combination skin. At other times, someone may notice that their pores appear larger as they age and lose skin elasticity.</span>
<h2><b>What Causes Enlarged Pores?</b></h2>
<span>Before exploring the steps to reduce the size of pores on the face, it is reasonable to understand what causes the pores to widen in the first place. These are the usual offenders:</span>
<ul><li aria-level="1"><span>Overproduction of oil</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Sun damage</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Dying and deterioration of collagen</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Improper skincare habits</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Dehydration</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Hormonal imbalances</span></li>
 	<li aria-level="1"><span>Genetics</span></li>
</ul>
<span>All these factors contribute to making your skin lose its elasticity and develop loose, flabby pores. When pores enlarge due to either of these causes, they tend to remain large until they are properly treated.</span>
<h2><b>Can You Shrink Pores?</b></h2>
<span>Although you can never alter the number of skin pores on your face, you can make them less visible and smaller. The process of learning how to shrink pores permanently involves regular skin care, a proper lifestyle, and, in some cases, referrals to professionals.</span>

<span>In the following sections, we will break down the most suitable choices for reducing pore size and discuss methods for temporarily making pores smaller over time.</span>
